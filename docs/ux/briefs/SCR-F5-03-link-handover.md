# SCR-F5-03 · Customer Link — Handover Pack

Phase-3 of the same URL: permanent read-only solar file — warranties, commissioning certificate, approvals, how-to-read-generation, referral ask and named contact.

**Module:** F5 (Customer link — the no-login tokenised customer journey) · **Personas:** Customer (an audience, never a role — no login, no account, no app) · **Context of use:** phone or desk, opened at handover and then returned to occasionally for years — the customer coming back two years later for a warranty document is the design case; view-only for life; slow connections are normal (text before weight).

## Entry & exit

Reached from: the same URL as the proposal and progress phases — the page becomes the document pack when the project reaches its terminal handed-over stage (`F5-70`); the rep's send at handover is a courtesy, not the mechanism — the customer's existing bookmark is the entry. Leads to: document downloads from the pack (`F5-71`); the referral ask's submission (`F5-72`); a link that cannot serve routes to the Link Failure Page (SCR-F5-04). No other exit is pinned by the PRD — designer decides, note the decision.

## Requirements (verbatim)

### From `docs/prd/foundations/F5-customer-link.md`

- **F5-07** (P0) — **The page is mobile-first, readable in one sitting, and usable on a slow connection — text before weight.** The reading order puts what decides the question first; nothing load-bearing waits on a heavy asset; and no state of the page is reachable only after a large download. Full parity at the large viewport is required, not a wider version of a reduced page (`F7-30`, `F7-31`).
- **F5-52** (P0) — **"Ask a question" is present in every phase of the link, not only on the proposal.** A customer's question does not stop being a question because the deal was won; the affordance is on the proposal page, the progress page and the handover page, in the same place, with the same behaviour.
- **F5-53** (P0) — **A question becomes a notification and a timeline entry on the tenant's side, and the reply is a call — the product sends no conversational reply on the tenant's behalf.** Verbatim: *"Questions land as notifications + timeline entries with reply-by-call workflow (app never sends)."* The customer's page acknowledges the question honestly — it states that the question has reached the company and that somebody will call — and it makes no claim about when, unless the tenant's own surface produced a commitment. There is no chat thread, no reply box and no message history on the customer's page, and the page says what happens next rather than implying a conversation that does not exist. *(The Q33 ruling's automatic transactional sends — link, confirmations, status updates — are one-way deal moments and do not touch this rule: no conversational reply is ever sent.)* _(non-UI half, build-side: question raises tenant-side notification and timeline entry; product never sends conversational reply; no chat thread exists — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-54** (P0) — **The customer can ask to be called, and that request reaches the queue as a customer-requested callback.** The affordance is the customer-side half of the callback path; the queue, its scheduling and its compliance checks are `modules/M07`'s (`M07-33`'s `callback_requested` trigger). **Under the requested-callback lane (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q30):** the customer's request made here is exactly the explicitly recorded, timestamped consent the lane requires — a customer who names a time outside the market's ordinary window **may be called at that time**, with the call opening by referencing this request and a single "stop" ending the lane; a request naming no time is honoured at the next lawful moment, and the page says which it will be. _(non-UI half, build-side: request queues as customer-requested callback with recorded, timestamped consent; named out-of-window time may be honoured; single stop ends lane — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-55** (P0) — **A named person, with a phone number, is on the page in every phase.** This is one of the three things the source says carries a customer through their most anxious moment, and it is also what the final step of the journey requires the handover to leave behind: *"the handover should leave them knowing exactly who to call."* The page never shows a generic company line where a named person exists.
- **F5-70** (P0) — **At handover the link becomes the document pack — the same URL, its third and final phase, and it is permanent (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q34).** Reaching the terminal handed-over stage is the project's act (`M08-46`); the transition of the page is this document's. Because the customer already holds the URL, handover requires no new share for the customer to reach their documents: the rep's send is a courtesy, not the mechanism. Post-handover the page is the customer's **permanent, read-only "solar file"** — documents, warranties and the referral ask (`F5-72`) — served for life, view-only: every respond scope is dead with its phase, and nothing on the page mutates anything ever again. _(non-UI half, build-side: permanent read-only solar file for life; respond scopes dead with phase; no new share needed to reach documents — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-71** (P0) — **The pack contains what the source names: the warranty documents, the commissioning certificate, the interconnection or metering approval, and how to read their generation.** The rows themselves, and which rows a market or segment omits, are pack data on the project's checklist (`M08-30`, `F1-22`); this page renders what the checklist assembled and never invents a row or presents an absent document as present.
- **F5-72** (P0) — **The referral is asked for here, at handover — while the roof is new and the first bill is about to arrive — and never six months later.** The ask is a first-class element of the handover page. What it produces is an attributed referral linking the referring customer to a new lead, visible on both records (`M02-16`, `M08-47`). **No monetary credit, no redemption and no balance exists in this release**, so the page promises none: it asks, it thanks, and it makes no offer it cannot keep.
- **F5-73** (P0) — **The handover leaves the customer knowing exactly who to call.** A named person with a number survives on the page after the project closes — the one live requirement the final step of the journey carries into this release.

_Annotation (not a rewrite of any quoted cell): the `F5-70` cell above is quoted verbatim and reads "every respond scope is dead with its phase, and **nothing on the page mutates anything ever again**". Three other P0 rows quoted on this same brief put controls on this page that each create a tenant-side record: `F5-52` (the question, which `F5-53` lands as a notification and a timeline entry), `F5-54` (the callback request, which reaches `modules/M07`'s queue as recorded, timestamped consent) and `F5-72` (the referral ask, "a first-class element of the handover page", which produces an attributed referral "visible on both records"). **As quoted, those rows and `F5-70`'s sentence conflict, and this brief says so rather than silently picking one** (`docs/ux/claude-design-context.md`: "If two requirements genuinely conflict on this screen, say so explicitly"). The reading the F5 document itself supports: **"respond scope" is `F5-21`'s term of art**, not a general word for any control — `F5-21` fixes the product-level scope set as "view the proposal · respond to the proposal (accept, negotiate, decline) · view progress · view the handover pack", with "effective rights = the token's scopes intersected with the link's current phase". What dies with the proposal phase is therefore accept/negotiate/decline. Ask a question, request a call and the referral ask are not respond-scope acts; each is carried onto this phase by its own P0 row, and `F5-52` says the affordance is on the handover page "in the same place, with the same behaviour". On that reading `F5-70`'s "nothing mutates" binds **the pack and the deal**: nothing here re-opens the proposal, edits a document, changes a project record or alters what the page displays. **Draw the three controls; draw nothing that edits the pack, the deal or the customer's own record.** This reading is the brief's — reconciling `F5-70`'s own sentence with `F5-52`/`F5-54`/`F5-72` in the source is held for the PRD owner, and `post-closure-view-only` below is worded to the reading, not to the literal cell._

## States

Three base states, then every screen-specific state from the slice and the rows:

- **loading** — nothing load-bearing waits on a heavy asset; the pack's contents are readable before any download (`F5-07`).
- **empty** — never fabricated: the pack renders what the checklist assembled and never invents a row or presents an absent document as present (`F5-71`); a row a market or segment omits is absent, not empty.
- **error** — a link that cannot serve routes to the honest failure rendering (SCR-F5-04); the handover page never enters its final phase with an incomplete pack (refused at the project, per F5 §F5.10 edge cases).
- **normal** — the permanent read-only solar file: documents, warranties, the referral ask, the named contact (`F5-70`–`F5-73`).
- **document-download** — a document from the pack being fetched/offered (`F5-71`).
- **referral-submitted-thanked** — the ask asks, it thanks, and it makes no offer it cannot keep: no credit, no redemption, no balance promised (`F5-72`).
- **post-closure-view-only** — after the project closes: every respond scope is dead with its phase — accept, negotiate and decline are gone, not disabled-looking (`F5-21`'s scope set, `F5-70`) — nothing on this page mutates the pack, the deal or the record it displays, and the named person with a number survives (`F5-70`, `F5-73`). The question, callback and referral controls are **not** respond-scope acts and stay live here by their own P0 rows — see the annotation above, which is where the tension with `F5-70`'s literal "nothing mutates anything ever again" is flagged.
- **question-acknowledged** — the question's honest acknowledgement, same place, same behaviour as every phase (`F5-52`, `F5-53`).
- **callback-requested** — the request-a-call acknowledgement; the page says which lawful moment the call will come (`F5-54`).

## Data volume

Design at the realistic pack size the checklist assembles (`F5-71`): the warranty documents (plural — panels, inverter and other equipment warranties are separate rows), the commissioning certificate, the interconnection or metering approval, and the how-to-read-generation material — with the understanding that which rows exist is market/segment pack data, so the layout must hold with rows absent as well as all present. Plus the referral ask, the named contact block and the question/callback affordances.

## Numbers carrying provenance

The slice rows put no money, energy or projection figure on this phase — the pack is documents, the referral ask and the contact. What the phase does show:

- Document dates and identifiers appearing on pack rows are the checklist's published facts (`F5-71`) — rendered as assembled, never invented; any user-visible number carries its F8 provenance tier in the design.
- The named contact's phone number is a contact detail, not a provenance-carrying figure (`F5-55`, `F5-73`).
- No referral credit, balance or amount exists anywhere on this page (`F5-72`).
