# SCR-M01-01 · Sign In

Front door: phone + OTP entry with Google Login alternative.

**Module:** M01 · **Personas:** All users; all studio personas · **Context of use:** the one screen every person passes through, on web and mobile equally. Employees arrive "Phone, almost always" (M01 §2); the owner is often on a laptop. Runs before roles exist — nothing here is permission-gated. The auth-rebuild acceptance line in M01 §M01.1 (owner ruling 2026-08-04) binds this screen: signed-out routing decided once for both platforms, success-dwell timing, visible resend feedback on every platform.

## Entry & exit

Reached from: any signed-out launch of the app or web product (signed-out routing decided once for both platforms — M01 §M01.1 acceptance, owner ruling 2026-08-04). Leads to: on success, tenant, user and role context is established (MS12-17) and the person lands on their role-decided home (M01 §M01.2, M01-17 — mechanics are `docs/prd/modules/M13-dashboards-and-reporting.md`'s). A first Google sign-in runs the linking flow onto the phone-identity account (M01-02). The path for a phone number with no account (hand-off to Company Signup, SCR-M01-02) was not pinned by PRD. **Decided in design 2026-08-27:** the front door never discloses whether a number has an account — an unknown number receives an OTP exactly as a known one does, and on successful verification the person is handed to `SCR-M01-02` with the verified number carried in. Two reasons: no account enumeration from a signed-out screen, and the verified phone identity (M01-18) is established before signup rather than after. A persistent **"Create a company account"** door also sits at the foot of the frame, reachable without typing a number.

**Further decisions made in design (2026-08-27) — later screens inherit them.**

1. **Policy constants carry no provenance tier; the sentence names whose rule it is.** 30 s, 3 per 15 min, 8 per day, 5 tries, the 15-minute lock, the 6-digit length — each was written down by somebody, not measured or estimated, so tiering them would be dishonest ("HelioGrid's rule"). The OTP itself is an identifier, not a quantity.
2. **No wall-clock time renders on this screen.** "Ask again at 16:44" would be a computed moment needing a tier, so every wait is stated as its policy duration instead.
3. **The primary is never gated on the field.** No disabled Send code, no advisory sentence at rest — an empty or malformed number is answered *on the field* when the control is pressed.
4. **A control that genuinely cannot act is absent, not greyed** (`MS12-17`, no dead controls) — the locked-number state carries no primary at all. Where a control cannot act but the reason matters, it is `aria-disabled`, stays focusable, and carries its reason.
5. **The Google control is text-only.** No Google asset is licensed into the system, and a character pressed into service as an icon is forbidden (`F7-19`/`F7-42`).
6. **Base `empty` is the normal frame** — on a single-record front door, empty *is* normal, and base `error` and `auth-error` are one surface drawn once. Both are resolutions, not omissions.
7. **`switch-discards-held-work` is the product's one deliberately unrecoverable act** (`F4-37`, the carve-out from `F4-21`). The count, who captured the work and when are named *before* the switch, upload is offered first and above the destructive control, the surface cannot be dismissed into silence, and the frame says in words that it cannot be undone.

**Build note — design-system gaps this screen found** are recorded once, in `packages/ui/CLAUDE.md` §"Known component gaps", which loads when anyone opens that folder. A screen brief is the wrong home for them: nobody building a component reads one.

### From `docs/prd/modules/M05-studio/11-shell-and-platform.md`

- **MS12-17** (P0) — Sign-in is the PLATFORM's: mobile OTP and Google, establishing tenant, user and role context (F2) — replacing the POC's mock two-phase login (S11-3a fixes `.52–.56`); no dead controls (S11-2.1 fixes `.55`). _(non-UI half, build-side: establishes tenant, user and role context; replaces mock login — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F4-37** (P0) — **On a shared device, tenant isolation beats convenience: a user switch discards work held for the previous user, and tells them before it happens.** Shared field phones are normal in this market. When a different user signs in on a device still holding photographs or submissions captured by another user and not yet uploaded, that held work is **discarded before any new data loads**, and the person whose work is being discarded is told what will be lost **before** the switch completes, with the chance to connect and upload first. This is the one **carve-out from `F4-21`**: everywhere else nothing a field user captured is ever unrecoverable, and here it is, deliberately — a rep must never reach another rep's customer photographs, and no device-held data survives the identity that captured it.

## States

Base: **loading** · **empty** · **error** (empty/error states carry F7's teaching-empty-state contract — M01 §M01.1 behavior detail).

Screen-specific:

- **normal** — phone entry with Google Login offered alongside Mobile OTP (M01-02); no password field exists anywhere (M01 §M01.1 acceptance); no dead controls (MS12-17).
- **otp-entry** — 6-digit OTP entry after send.
- **otp-sent** — code dispatched on the SMS channel, which is the only delivery channel; nothing is retried behind the user's back (M01-03, owner ruling 2026-08-06). *(This state previously read "code dispatched on the primary SMS channel" — there is no secondary channel for it to be primary to.)*
- **otp-auto-read** — OTP entry auto-reads the code where the platform allows (M01 §M01.1 behavior detail).
- **wrong-code** — says what happened and what to do next (M01 §M01.1 behavior detail).
- **expired-code** — OTP failure state; says what happened and what to do next.
- **resend-cooldown** — visible resend control under its **30 s** cooldown (M01-04, owner ruling 2026-08-06). The countdown this state draws is 30 s; the 45 s alternative is superseded (M01-04). **This cooldown does not apply once a hard delivery failure is confirmed (M01-03, M01-04, owner ruling 2026-08-06)** — a confirmed hard failure releases it immediately, so this state does not render there; it governs only the ordinary case, where the message may still be in flight. *(This state previously drew the 30 s countdown with no exception stated.)*
- **call-me-instead** — voice-OTP option offered when the code still does not arrive; it is **user-initiated**, not a system fallback, and is retained on that basis (M01-03, owner ruling 2026-08-06). It is offered **immediately in the delivery-failed-retry-later state** as well, when the SMS rail confirms a hard failure (M01-03, owner ruling 2026-08-06). *(This state previously named only the code-does-not-arrive trigger.)*
- **delivery-failed-retry-later** — loud failure: SMS delivery failed, it is said plainly, and the front door never pretends to be waiting (M01-03, M01 §M01.1 edge list). *(This state was named **all-channels-failed-retry-later** before owner ruling 2026-08-06; there is one delivery channel now, so there are no "all channels" to fail.)* **On a confirmed hard delivery failure the resend control is available immediately in this state — no cooldown countdown, nothing to wait out — and the user-initiated "call me instead" voice OTP is offered here too (M01-03, M01-04, owner ruling 2026-08-06).** M01-04's anti-abuse caps are unchanged and still bind: if a cap is already reached at that moment, the cap governs and this state says so honestly (M01-04). **What the message SAYS in that confirmed-hard-failure case is not pinned by PRD — designer decides, and records the chosen wording back into this line** (an implementation-time screen-copy item, so classed 2026-08-06: no owner ruling is owed on it, none is pending, and nothing in the product waits on it). **Written in design 2026-08-27:** the heading is **"The code did not send"**, the reason line *"The SMS network turned it away"*, and the wait line *"Nothing is on its way, so there is nothing to wait for."* Resend is live beside it with no countdown, and the voice route sits next to it. The word "later" appears nowhere, which is the hard-failure ruling's one constraint. Where an `M01-04` request cap is already reached at that moment the cap governs instead, in its own state, and the message says so. **The one constraint on the words is already fixed by that ruling: the copy must not say "later" while the retry is live** — so `M01-03`'s *"retry-later"* wording is superseded in that case and cannot be the copy; the state's id is a label that follows the copy and never leads it, which is why it is not renamed here. Where the failure is **not** a confirmed hard one, nothing is superseded: `M01-03`'s plain retry-later message stands as written and the ordinary 30 s cooldown of **resend-cooldown** governs. *(This state previously drew only the loud retry-later message and said nothing about the resend control or the voice option inside it, and until 2026-08-16 opened "loud failure with a plain retry-later message" in the same line that makes the resend control available immediately — a contradiction to whoever draws it.)*
- **verified-success-dwell** — the confirmation beat after the code is accepted and before the post-auth handoff to the role-decided home. On a bad connection this is the only thing telling the user it actually worked, so the moment is drawn, not skipped. **The dwell duration is not pinned by PRD — designer decides, and records the choice here** (owner ruling 2026-08-07; the same class as the hard-failure copy above — this was never an owner preference, it is a value written when the screen is designed). Pick one duration, draw it, and write it into this line so the other platform inherits it rather than choosing a second answer; whatever is chosen applies to **both** platforms, which is what the rebuild acceptance line means by "success-dwell timing". **Decided in design 2026-08-27: 1.2 s, on both platforms, and the beat names its destination ("Taking you to My Day").** Long enough to read as confirmation on a slow hand-off, short enough that nobody waits on it — a silent beat is indistinguishable from a stall. *(State added by the readiness pass, 2026-08-07: the rebuild acceptance line named success-dwell timing as binding, but no state carried it and no duration existed anywhere, so the moment would simply never have been drawn.)*
- **number-locked-15min** — 3 consecutive OTP invalidations lock the number for 15 min with an honest message (M01-04). **The lock is the SMS channel only (owner ruling 2026-08-27):** this state renders no code entry and no resend, states what paused and how long, and **keeps Continue with Google live** as the way in — the message says so rather than leaving the working door unmentioned. *(Ruled after the state was first drawn: the frame claimed neither reading, and now carries this one.)*
- **auth-error** — generic authentication failure, honestly stated.
- **google-link-first-sign-in** — the first Google sign-in runs the linking flow onto the same phone-identity account (M01-02).
- **switch-discards-held-work** — a different user is signing in on a device still holding un-uploaded photographs or submissions: what will be lost is named BEFORE the switch completes, with the chance to connect and upload first; on confirm the held work is discarded before any new data loads (F4-37, the one carve-out from F4-21).

*State removed by owner ruling 2026-08-06, recorded not deleted:* **channel-fallback-fired** — which read "automatic fallback channel fired on delivery failure or the 30 s timeout (M01-03)". The system uses no fallback mechanism, so this state cannot occur and must not be drawn. The **resend-cooldown** state above was left untouched by the fallback's removal and is the whole of the recovery path the system offers, alongside the user-initiated **call-me-instead**. *(That sentence previously read "The **resend-cooldown** state above is unchanged and is now the whole of the recovery path the system offers, alongside the user-initiated **call-me-instead**." — no longer sayable as written: the same day's hard-failure ruling has since amended that state, releasing the cooldown immediately when a hard delivery failure is confirmed, and leaves the removed fallback untouched.)*

## Data volume

A single-record form: one phone number (normalized to the market's phone specification), one 6-digit OTP, and two delivery channels in play — SMS, plus the user-initiated voice OTP; no automatic fallback channel exists (owner ruling 2026-08-06 — this sentence previously read "up to three delivery channels in play (SMS → fallback messaging channel → voice)"). Design for all launch languages — signup and OTP screens exist in every launch language and default to the device locale (M01 §M01.1 localization notes).

## Numbers carrying provenance

Every rendered number carries its F8 provenance tier in the design. This screen's user-visible numbers are product policy constants, shown honestly per M01-04: the resend cooldown countdown (30 s — owner ruling 2026-08-06; the 45 s alternative is superseded), the request caps (3 per 15 min, 8 per day), the 5-failed-attempts invalidation, the 15-minute lock timer, and the 6-digit OTP length. No money, business quantity or business date renders here.
