# SCR-M01-05 · Business Profile

Single write-point for logo, tax registration, address, bank details; skippable onboarding step and later prompt-point.

**Module:** M01 · **Personas:** EPC Owner · **Context of use:** appears **three times** in the product's life — as a skippable step during company onboarding (laptop-leaning, mobile-capable — M01 §2); as the in-context prompt-point fired when the first proposal is about to be sent (M01-24, M01-29 context: "configure in context, not in a settings maze"); and as the settings screen for revisiting (M01-29: "Settings screens exist for revisiting, not for setup"), which the owner ruling that put the tenant's segment and typical-size declarations here (`M01-23`) made load-bearing. *(This line read "twice" and named only the first two until 2026-08-28; Entry & exit named three all along.)* Permission: `F2.M01.manage-tenant-settings` (EPC Owner); settings changes are audit events (M01 §M01.3 permissions).

## Entry & exit

Reached from: the onboarding sequence after "What do you sell?" (M01 §M01.3 behavior detail); later, the prompt-point when the first proposal is about to be sent — prompt-points offer *do it now inline* or *keep skipping* (M01 §M01.3 behavior detail); and settings for revisiting ("Settings screens exist for revisiting, not for setup" — M01-29 context). Leads to: on skip during onboarding, the next onboarding step (the skippable invite step, then the two-door landing — M01 §M01.3 behavior detail); on save, back to wherever the prompt fired. Consumers (proposal, agent script, customer link, invoice) read this one profile by reference — they never re-ask (M01-31). **This screen is also where the tenant's segment and typical-system-size declarations are edited after onboarding (owner ruling 2026-08-28).** `M01-23` collects them once on `SCR-M01-04` and stores them; it named no later surface, so that screen's *change this later* line and its failed-save escape had nothing to point at. They sit here for the reason the rest of this profile does — one write-point the consumers read by reference and never re-ask (`M01-31`). The size is declared in **`kWp`**, tiered `estimated · Declared by you`, and changing it re-seeds only what comes next: a sent proposal keeps its prices. Their permission is this screen's own — `F2.M01.manage-tenant-settings`, and the change is an audit event like every other settings change here.

In onboarding the act leads on to the invite step (`SCR-M01-07`), then the two-door landing; the onboarding life has no back control and no step counter (decision 5).

**Further decisions made in design (2026-08-28) — later screens inherit them.**

1. **Five collapsible sections in place, never five rows that each open a sheet.** Three shapes were possible for eleven fields. *All of it at once* is the whole control surface (`F7-34` forbids it); *five rows opening sheets* is exactly the settings maze `M01-29` exists to replace — the sheet grammar (`F7-21`) is for editing something whose context is elsewhere, and here the profile **is** the screen. Collapsible sections, one open: the reader sees all five parts and where each stands, and meets one part's controls.
2. **One forward act, and no separate Skip.** With nothing filled in, *skip* and *save* are the same write — two controls would be two labels for one act, and a skip that discarded typed input would be a destructive control on an onboarding step (`N8`). The act stays live with nothing answered (`M06-22` killed the Next-disabled rule).
3. **The two declarations are present in the onboarding appearance too, as a read-back.** `M01-23` puts them here for editing *after* onboarding, and the literal reading would hide the part during the step — but `SCR-M01-04`'s line *you can change both in your company profile, the next step* is a promise made one tap earlier. The part stands **Answered**; nothing is re-asked.
4. **The prompt-point is this screen's tax-registration field set inside `EditorSurface`, over the proposal it is holding up.** One field set, two containers — which is why the parts are authored as field sets rather than page sections, and why `F7-21`'s sheet-on-mobile / panel-on-desktop switch is performed by the system rather than restated here.
5. **The part titles are the profile's parts, not the brief's field list** — *Logo and company name · Address · Tax registration · Bank details · What you sell*. The first joins two facts because they are one act: the two things a document shows at the top.
6. **A logo's limits are stated before the upload, not only inside the refusal**, and the refusal names **every** limit missed — a file can be small enough and still too low-resolution, and a refusal mentioning only size sends the owner back with the wrong fix.
7. **A limit is not a value and carries no tier.** A size ceiling, a minimum edge, a format's character count — a tier says how a value was arrived at, and a limit was not arrived at, it was set. Recurs on every screen that states a constraint.
8. **Density follows the life, not the screen (`F7-17`, owner ruling 2026-08-28).** The onboarding and prompt appearances are **expressive** — onboarding and authentication are on that list. The **settings appearance is `functional`**, because `F7-17` names *settings and administration* on the functional list and the method is to pick the mode from what the screen **is**. The corridor argument that carries the onboarding appearance does not reach the settings one: it sits in no corridor, it is one destination among many. `SCR-SHELL-03` used the same method to rule its inbox *not* settings; this life is. Spacing and radius tighten on those two frames only — the 44px floor is the components' and does not move, and capability is untouched.
9. **The settings appearance reuses `SCR-SHELL-01`'s shell exactly** — four destinations, `Home · Leads · Proposals · More` (`F6-22`), with the pill footer's add action; settings is reached from **More**, never as a rail entry. Three things change with the chrome and nothing else: the wordmark goes, the corridor's act becomes this screen's own `Save changes`, and at 1536 the list starts at the header title's edge, in the app's grid, with the filled letterhead beside it. **Capability is identical across all three lives.**

**Owner rulings in the redraw of 2026-10-12 — the build follows these, never the older form.**

10. **The same flat list at both widths; at 1536 a letterhead preview sits beside it.** The index and panel are gone. The preview is decorative, holds no control, and shows only what the fields hold.
11. **Bank details hold four facts** (`M06-17`), in this order: `Bank name` · `Account holder` · `Account number` · the pack's bank-routing identifier.
12. **The address is three fields: `Street address` · `City` · `PIN code`.** The city is read back with `From your signup` and nothing more — the market comes from the phone number the company signed up with, never from the city. The PIN code is stored on the profile. No state field is drawn.
13. **One short format line serves the help and the error** — `15 characters · like 27ABCDE1234F1Z5` in the India pack — and the screen never counts the characters typed. A field that is optional on its own says so in its label: `GSTIN (optional)`.
14. **No line at the act says the step is skippable**: a live act with nothing filled in is what skippable looks like. Only the re-seed scope, the cost of an undeclared value and a save that failed keep a line there.
15. **The prompt at the first send is built with the send** (`T-M06-022`): this screen's tax part, mounted in `EditorSurface` over the proposal. `m-prompt` and `d-prompt` are proven there, where a proposal exists to hold up.
16. **The bank code's label is the market pack's**, as the tax registration's is: `IFSC` in the India pack, never a word of the screen's own.
17. **Bank details typed half way are kept as typed.** The part reads `Not added` until all four facts exist, and the bank details' prompt stays owed until then. The half is on this screen's own read alone: a proposal, an invoice or a customer link reads all four facts or none.
18. **A save that fails keeps everything typed on screen**, with a retry and a way on without saving. `m-save-failed` draws it: one line at the act, `Not saved · our end · nothing typed is lost`, then `Try again` and `Continue without it`; the settings life shows the same line at `Save changes` and no second act.
19. **`skipped` has no way in yet**: a recorded step is never resumed, and no screen offers a way back into setup. The state is built and waits for that screen.
20. **The settings life waits on the settings list** its back control returns to; that list is drawn before this life is built. Drawn 2026-10-13 as three frames on the `SCR-SHELL-01` board: the page is titled `Settings`, its row for this screen reads `Business profile`, and `T-M01-005f` builds it under `More` on the web and both phones.
21. **A kind of business has one name on every screen.** The three card titles here are the ones `SCR-M01-04` shows — `Residential` · `Commercial & industrial (C&I)` · `Both`; the card lines, the group's name (`What you mostly sell`) and the size's hint are this screen's own, as drawn. The board's three titles are to be corrected to match before the part that draws the cards.
22. **The account holder opens holding the company name.** A holder that still equals the company name, with the other three bank facts empty, is not a bank fact: nothing is written for it and the part reads `Not added`.
23. **The address reads `Added` once the street address is filled.** A PIN code alone leaves it at the city read back from signup.
24. **The three examples inside fields are screen copy**, as drawn — `411038`, `HDFC Bank`, `HDFC0001234` — like company signup's examples; `docs/tasks/deferred.md` D207 holds their move to the market pack.
25. **An open part shows no standing, unless it owes something.** Its fields say what it holds; a part that owes something (`m-failed-save`, `Not declared`) opens itself and keeps its word while open. `m-normal` and the two settings frames, which keep `Added` on the open part, are to be corrected to match before the parts that build them.
26. **The logo's capture area lands whole with the logo** — its words, its hint by device, its refusal and the upload together; until then the first part holds the company name alone.
27. **A field reports what is typed as it is typed, and can be emptied.** The board marks the fields commit-on-blur; the shared field's commit-once mode never commits an empty box, so a typed address could not be taken out again. Nothing is saved before the act either way.
28. **The typical size has `+` and `−` steppers on this screen, 0.5 kWp a press**, as drawn; the step before keeps its field without them. The field holds two decimals, the stored column's precision, where the board passes one.
29. **A word two screens share is the catalog's one word in every language.** *Declared by you*, *Typical system size* and the tier word read here as the step before and every tier line show them; the board's two language frames are not read (`.claude/rules/ui-adherence.md`, *Screens*).
30. **The size's hint stands under an empty size field too.** `m-failed-save` draws the field with no hint; *The size you sell most often* helps most where nothing is declared, so it is built in every state, and the frame is to be corrected to match.
31. **From 968 px both halves are drawn, sized from the 1536 frame as shares of the window**: the letterhead's track 44% of it, never under 472 nor over 672; the list's side padding 6.25% of it, never under 32 nor over 96. No second breakpoint.
32. **The letterhead's address is one line**: the street when there is one, then the city and the PIN code — `Plot 14, Baner Road, Pune 411045`; with no street, `Pune 411045`.
33. **The letterhead names the bank only when all four bank facts are held**, as the part's `Added` is (ruling 17): the bank's name and the account's last four characters — `HDFC Bank · account ending 4821`; until then a bar.
34. **A size of the letterhead that is off the spacing scale takes the nearest token**: the sheet's gap 28 → `sp-6`, the bars 10 → `sp-2` high and `sp-2` apart, the list's top padding 56 → `sp-12`. The tile's 56, the sheet's 440 and a bar's 140 are the drawing's own sizes.
35. **An emptied name or city is saved as it was read.** A company always has both, so the field may be emptied while typing and the save still sends what the profile held. Each field stops at the length the api holds for its fact.
36. **A tax number that reads well only in capitals is taken in capitals.** The owner who typed the right number in small letters is not told to check its format.
37. **The lede names the step before only for answers that arrived missing.** An answer the owner takes out on this screen marks the part `Not declared` and keeps the line at the act; the lede stays as drawn on `m-default`.
38. **A bank fact typed half way makes the step `completed`**, as a street or a PIN code does. With nothing of this screen's own typed the step is `skipped`.
39. **`Continue without it` puts this step off for the session and records nothing.** The next sign-in asks for the step again.
40. **The live check reads the api's own market pack**, so the field and the save never disagree about a tax number.
41. **A tax number typed malformed takes nothing away.** It is never stored, and a number already stored stays as it is; a stored number the owner empties is taken out. A number that stays stored completes the step, as a typed one does. The account holder that still holds the name read at arrival, beside no other bank fact, is nothing typed, also when the company was renamed on this screen.
42. **Both answers given here after `Finish later` answer the step before too.** `SCR-M01-04`'s `Finish later` opens this screen with its step unrecorded; a save here that sends a kind and a size records that step `completed`, so the next sign-in does not ask again. A save without both leaves it unrecorded, and the next sign-in asks.
43. **The cost of an undeclared answer keeps each screen's own words.** `Market defaults until you set these` here and `Until you finish, proposals start without your answers.` on `SCR-M01-04` both stay as drawn.
44. **The device checks a logo's width before it uploads.** `At least 400 px wide` is one rule the screen reads; the api checks the file's type and size, and reads no image's width.
45. **A file of another type is refused alone, in one line: `Needs a PNG or JPG`.** It is not measured — the right file is another file, with its own size and width — and an image whose width no device can read is refused the same way. No frame draws this refusal; it stands where the drawn one does.
46. **A refusal names only the limits the image missed**, each as a whole line: its size and its width when both are missed (as drawn), its size alone, or its width alone.
47. **A refused image's size is printed in megabytes to one decimal, rounded up**, so a file one byte over never reads as the limit (`2.1 MB · needs 2 MB or less`). `MB` and `px` are units and read the same in every language.
48. **The letterhead's tile shows the logo's picture**, fitted inside the tile, and the word `Logo` until the screen holds one. A company with no picture shows no initial: the preview shows only what the fields hold (ruling 10).
49. **The capture area's hint follows the pointer.** Where a finger chooses, `Tap to choose a file`; where a pointer can drop a file, `Choose a file or drop one here`; each is followed by the limits. `d-default`, which draws the finger's line, is to be corrected to match.
50. **No control takes a logo out.** Choosing another file replaces the held one, as `m-normal` draws.
51. **A logo is stored by the act, not when it is chosen.** A chosen image is checked and held on the device and shows at once as `Your logo`; `Save and continue` stores it and then saves the profile with it. So an upload has no look of its own: the act's busy look is its look, and one that fails is the failed save (`m-save-failed`), everything kept. An image chosen and then abandoned stores nothing.
52. **An image over the size limit is refused with its measured size, never shrunk** — on a phone too. A logo is a made image; re-encoding it without telling its owner would change what every document prints.
53. **An image the api reads as another type than the device declared is refused in the type's line**, `Needs a PNG or JPG`, and that save writes nothing; the next press saves without it.
54. **The settings life is titled by its row: `Business profile`**, on `m-settings` and `d-settings` — the word a person taps on the settings list is the page they land on (owner ruling, `T-M01-005o` design check). The setup life keeps `Your company profile`.

Inside the shell the top bar and the pill footer already hold the screen's edges; an act pinned between them leaves the list too little room to show an open part. At 1536 the act stays under the list region, as `d-settings` draws it: the list fits, and the act is always in view (owner ruling, `T-M01-005o` design check).

## Requirements (verbatim)

### From `docs/prd/modules/M01-onboarding-and-tenant-config.md`

- **M01-24** (P0) — **The company profile is skippable and prompted in context.** Logo, tax registration, address, bank details are a skippable onboarding step — prompted later, **when the first proposal is about to be sent** (the moment they are actually needed). Tax registrations stay empty until that first proposal; the registration *types* that exist come from the tenant market's `pack.tax` (F1-13).
- **M01-25** (P0) — **Tax-registration entry validates live, explains the format, and allows skip.** A malformed registration is explained against the market's format (from `pack.tax`), never silently rejected and never a hard wall — skip remains available until the send moment forces the prompt again.
- **M01-31** (P0) — **One Business profile screen feeds many places.** Company name, logo, address and tax registration are asked once and used by the proposal, the agent's script, the customer link and the invoice — never asked twice by different surfaces. _(non-UI half, build-side: single write-point for identity facts; proposal, agent, link, invoice consume by reference — for awareness, not for drawing)_

## States

Base: **loading** · **empty** · **error**. **`empty` resolves to the default frame and gets no separate drawing (settled 2026-08-28).** A profile with *nothing known* cannot occur: `M01-01` captures company name, owner name and city at signup and admits no other tenant-creating path — `M01-08` sends a known phone to login on the existing account, and `M01-09`'s request-to-join lands inside a tenant that already has a profile. So the emptiest this screen ever is, is the profile with nothing **the owner has added yet** — no logo, no tax registration, no bank details — with the name and city read back from signup, and that is the default. Same resolution `SCR-M01-03` and `SCR-M01-04` reached, argued from this screen's own facts.

**loading** fetches the record alone: the wordmark, heading and lede draw at once, a bar stands for each part, and the act is off with its reason stated, `Profile still loading`, and stays focusable. **error** — the profile could not be fetched — is not a wall: `Try again` leads, and `Continue without it` follows, since nothing is loaded to save.

Drawn at 375 only (at 1536 the phone's answer holds at width): `loading`, `error`, `skipped`, `normal`, `logo-invalid-limits-stated`, `declarations-edited-reseed-scope`, `save-failed`, `arrived-from-a-failed-save`.

Screen-specific:

- **skipped** — the whole step skipped during onboarding; tax registrations stay empty until the first proposal (M01-24); a tenant with no config at all breaks nothing (M01 §M01.3 context, M01-28 — not a row of this slice).
- **tax-format-invalid-explained** — a malformed registration is explained against the market's format (from `pack.tax`), never silently rejected, never a hard wall; skip stays available (M01-25).
- **prompt-at-first-proposal-send** — the later prompt-point fires when the first proposal is about to be sent, inline, offering do-it-now or keep-skipping (M01-24; M01 §M01.3 acceptance).
- **normal** — the profile filled and saved: logo, the market's tax registration(s), address, bank details, and the two declarations `M01-23` places here. The main frame of the screen, and the one the other states are departures from. *(Added 2026-08-28 — this list named four edge states and no default; every neighbouring brief names one.)*
- **logo-invalid-limits-stated** — a logo uploaded at the profile step is invalid → validated on upload with the actual limits stated (M01 §M01.3 edge list, the `S6B.wrong.5` pattern).
- **declarations-edited-reseed-scope** — the segment and typical system size (`kWp`) edited after onboarding, with the scope of the change stated on the surface: it re-seeds only what comes next, and **a sent proposal keeps its prices** (`M01-23`; the price-book law of `F1`). The size carries its tier — `estimated · Declared by you` — wherever it renders, exactly as on `SCR-M01-04`. *(Added 2026-08-28 with the ruling that placed the declarations here, which reached Entry & exit and not this list.)*
- **save-failed** — the act was pressed and the save got no answer: every typed value stays where it was typed, no part closes, and one line at the act says so, with a retry first and a way on without saving second (ruling 18). Not *arrived-from-a-failed-save*, which is the step before failing.
- **arrived-from-a-failed-save** — reached by `SCR-M01-04`'s *Finish later* route, where a save of those two declarations failed. The screen says what is still undeclared rather than presenting an empty field as a choice, so the owner does not have to remember what the previous screen lost. *(Added 2026-08-28: placing the declarations here made this an entry point, and an entry with no state is how a route arrives somewhere that does not expect it.)*

## Data volume

One company-profile record: one logo, the market's tax-registration types (from `pack.tax` — the set and formats are market-pack data; the module never names one market's tax ID in UI copy, M01 §M01.3 localization notes), one address with its PIN code, one set of bank details — four facts (`M06-17`).

## Numbers carrying provenance

Every rendered **quantity** carries its F8 provenance tier in the design. **The two values this screen collects are identifiers, not quantities, so neither carries a tier (owner ruling 2026-08-28):** the tax registration and the bank details are tenant-entered identity facts consumed by reference everywhere (`M01-31`). Applying a tier to one would be nonsense — `estimated` on a registration number claims a guess nobody made, `measured` claims a verification nobody performed. **What each one does owe is a plain statement of what was checked**, beside the value, which is `F8-03`'s own mechanism: `M01-25`'s live check tests the **market pack's format** and nothing else, so the screen says the format was checked and the issuing authority was not. Never a fifth tier and never a parallel standing — `F8-03` forbids both by name. The one true quantity this screen holds is the typical system size (`kWp`, `M01-23`), which keeps the tier it was declared with on `SCR-M01-04`: `estimated · Declared by you`. A logo's stated limits are rules, not values, and carry nothing. No computed money or date renders here.
