# SCR-F5-01 · Customer Link — Proposal

Phase-1 tokenised page: priced proposal with honesty labels, 3D roof view, financing, Accept / negotiate / decline and Ask a question, read in one evening sitting on a phone.

**Module:** F5 (Customer link — the no-login tokenised customer journey) · **Personas:** Customer (an audience, never a role — no login, no account, no app; residential homeowner and C&I buyer served by one design) · **Context of use:** phone, in the evening, one decisive sitting, possibly with a spouse; slow connections are normal (text before weight); the customer holds only a link and no credential.

## Entry & exit

Reached from: the tokenised link minted when a rep shares the proposal (the share act mints the link — `F5-20`, `M06-53`); the customer arrives from the message carrying the link (tenant's connected transactional channel, or the composed copy-paste fallback), from a copied link or a scanned QR (`MS9-14`), or from a deep link (`MS9-15`). Leads to: the read-only Customer 3D View (SCR-F5-05) behind the "View in 3D" button (`F5-33`); the SLD page only when a real SLD exists (`MS9-25`); the offered document download (`F5-39`); the honest Link Failure Page (SCR-F5-04) when the link cannot serve (`F5-25`); and — at the same URL, when the deal is won — the Progress phase (SCR-F5-02, `F5-19`). Back navigation returns to where the reader came from (`MS9-25`).

## Requirements (verbatim)

### From `docs/prd/foundations/F5-customer-link.md`

- **F5-07** (P0) — **The page is mobile-first, readable in one sitting, and usable on a slow connection — text before weight.** The reading order puts what decides the question first; nothing load-bearing waits on a heavy asset; and no state of the page is reachable only after a large download. Full parity at the large viewport is required, not a wider version of a reduced page (`F7-30`, `F7-31`).
- **F5-32** (P0) — **The proposal page shows exactly what the source names, and this is the moment of maximum attention.** System size, annual generation and monthly savings · total price, incentive and what the customer actually pays · payback period · **a 3D view of their own roof** · financing options · **Accept** · **Ask a question**. It is opened once, properly, probably in the evening, probably on a phone, possibly with a spouse — so the page is ordered for a single decisive reading rather than for exploration.
- **F5-33** (P0) — **The 3D view of the customer's own roof is the differentiating moment and is a first-class element of this page, not an attachment — shipped as a "View in 3D" button inside the proposal link (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q27).** The source: *"this is the moment that separates you from a PDF emailed by a competitor."* The model, its captures and its read-only presentation are `modules/M05`'s (`M05-55`); this page renders the recommended design's live 3D view behind its **"View in 3D"** button, read-only, with every control absent — **no second link exists**; captures/pictures remain the fallback where the live view cannot serve. - **F5-35** (P0) — **The customer sees one recommended system by default.** Where a lead carries variants, exactly one is marked recommended in the studio and that is what this page renders; variants appear only where the designer deliberately added them for a price-sensitive or undecided customer. The page never asks the customer to choose between engineering options they have no basis to evaluate.
- **F5-36** (P0) — **Every figure on this page carries its provenance tier, and every energy figure additionally carries its source label naming the database.** The tiers are the canonical four and no surface invents a fifth (`F8-02`, `F8-03`); the energy labels are fixed copy (`F8-08`) and the source is never switched silently (`F8-09`). Labels are persistent readable content beside the number — never a tooltip, never a colour, never a footnote (`F8-07`).
- **F5-37** (P0) — **The three disclosure lines render on this page verbatim wherever they apply:** the indicative line on any proposal built without a design; the remote-survey basis line where the design was built on imagery rather than an on-site measurement; and the projection label with its assumptions on every multi-year financial figure. The source's competitive reading is carried as the rationale, not as decoration: *"Competitors print estimates as certainties. When a customer compares three proposals and only yours admits which numbers are estimated, that reads as confidence, not weakness."*
- **F5-39** (P0) — **The web page is the path to the number; the document is an artifact.** Verbatim: *"The customer link always renders the proposal as web — PDF is an artifact, never the only path to the number."* A customer on a slow connection or an old device reads every figure, disclosure and label on the page without downloading anything; the document is offered, never required. Where a document render fails it is retried once and then fails with a notification to the operator — it never leaves the customer with a broken link to a missing file. _(non-UI half, build-side: document render retries once then notifies operator; web page, not PDF, is the path of record — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-42** (P1) — **Financing options render as labelled projections with their assumptions, never as an offer.** The instalment arithmetic is the proposal's (`M06-40`); this page renders it as a projection carrying its assumptions (`F8-23`), and makes no statement about eligibility, approval or availability — there is no financing marketplace in this release.
- **F5-43** (P0) — **Acceptance happens in exactly one way: the customer taps Accept on their link.** No verbal agreement on a call, no note from a rep, no automated caller's report of enthusiasm, and no operator action anywhere in the product constitutes the customer's acceptance. The decision itself is theirs to make in one of three ways — accept, go quiet, or say no — and only the first is an act the product records as acceptance. _(non-UI half, build-side: no verbal agreement, rep note or operator act ever records acceptance — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-44** (P0) — **OTP-at-accept ships DEFAULT OFF at launch (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q42): no value threshold is set, and the challenge is a per-tenant enable in settings.** Where a tenant enables it, Accept is challenged with a one-time verification of the person accepting; any tenant-set threshold is **tenant configuration denominated in the tenant's currency** (`F1-07`); the delivery capability is a market-pack reference rail behind a vendor-neutral port (`F1-43`). Reading stays frictionless — the challenge exists only at the commitment, never at the reading. **The C&I acceptance risk is stated honestly:** with the challenge off, a high-value Accept is evidenced by named-link open-attribution and the acceptance record (`F5-26`, `F5-27`, `F5-46`) rather than by a verified OTP — a residual risk the owner accepted for launch; **revisit post-launch** is the ruling's own trigger. No product default threshold exists and no pack key carries one. _(non-UI half, build-side: OTP-at-accept ships default OFF; per-tenant enable; any threshold is tenant-currency config; delivery via vendor-neutral market-pack rail — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-47** (P0) — **Accept is never trusted from the link alone — the server re-checks before it records anything.** It confirms that the version being accepted is the current one and is not stale (money never renders or commits while stale — `F8-12`), that the deal is not already won or lost, and that the challenge of `F5-44` has been satisfied where the threshold requires it. A re-check that fails states plainly what happened and what the customer should do; it never records a partial acceptance. _(non-UI half, build-side: server revalidates version currency, staleness, deal state and challenge before recording; never a partial acceptance — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-48** (P0) — **Acknowledgement is immediate, and it lives on the link itself — with the instant message now sent automatically (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q33).** The customer expects to know within seconds that the tap registered — *"not a silence that makes them wonder if the tap registered"*. The page's own confirmation state is the acknowledgement of record: it changes at once, states what has been accepted and what happens next, and depends on no message. The instant confirmation message the source describes **sends automatically from the tenant's connected transactional channel** the moment Accept records; where no channel is connected, the composed message a person sends is the fallback — and the page state already did the acknowledging. _(non-UI half, build-side: instant confirmation message auto-sends from connected transactional channel; copy-paste fallback; page state is acknowledgement of record — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-52** (P0) — **"Ask a question" is present in every phase of the link, not only on the proposal.** A customer's question does not stop being a question because the deal was won; the affordance is on the proposal page, the progress page and the handover page, in the same place, with the same behaviour.
- **F5-53** (P0) — **A question becomes a notification and a timeline entry on the tenant's side, and the reply is a call — the product sends no conversational reply on the tenant's behalf.** Verbatim: *"Questions land as notifications + timeline entries with reply-by-call workflow (app never sends)."* The customer's page acknowledges the question honestly — it states that the question has reached the company and that somebody will call — and it makes no claim about when, unless the tenant's own surface produced a commitment. There is no chat thread, no reply box and no message history on the customer's page, and the page says what happens next rather than implying a conversation that does not exist. *(The Q33 ruling's automatic transactional sends — link, confirmations, status updates — are one-way deal moments and do not touch this rule: no conversational reply is ever sent.)* _(non-UI half, build-side: question raises tenant-side notification and timeline entry; product never sends conversational reply; no chat thread exists — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-54** (P0) — **The customer can ask to be called, and that request reaches the queue as a customer-requested callback.** The affordance is the customer-side half of the callback path; the queue, its scheduling and its compliance checks are `modules/M07`'s (`M07-33`'s `callback_requested` trigger). **Under the requested-callback lane (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q30):** the customer's request made here is exactly the explicitly recorded, timestamped consent the lane requires — a customer who names a time outside the market's ordinary window **may be called at that time**, with the call opening by referencing this request and a single "stop" ending the lane; a request naming no time is honoured at the next lawful moment, and the page says which it will be. _(non-UI half, build-side: request queues as customer-requested callback with recorded, timestamped consent; named out-of-window time may be honoured; single stop ends lane — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-55** (P0) — **A named person, with a phone number, is on the page in every phase.** This is one of the three things the source says carries a customer through their most anxious moment, and it is also what the final step of the journey requires the handover to leave behind: *"the handover should leave them knowing exactly who to call."* The page never shows a generic company line where a named person exists.

## States

Three base states, then every screen-specific state from the slice and the rows:

- **loading** — hydration shows a loading state rather than a blank page (`MS9-15`).
- **empty** — no populated proposal ever renders empty to a valid recipient; the honest form of "nothing to show" is the incomplete-design message (`MS9-15`), never a blank page.
- **error** — a link that cannot serve routes to the honest failure rendering (SCR-F5-04, `F5-25`); errors on this page state plainly what happened (e.g. the Accept re-check refusal of `F5-47`).
- **normal** — the full proposal reading: figures with honesty labels, roof view, financing, Accept, Ask a question (`F5-32`).
- **no-approved-design-indicative** — proposal built without a design: the 3D model is absent and the indicative disclosure line renders verbatim (`F5-37`).
- **incomplete-design** — a valid link to an incomplete design gets an honest message, not "invalid link" (`MS9-15`).
- **deep-link** — a deep link never crashes (`MS9-15`).
- **3d-view-open** — the read-only 3D view open behind the "View in 3D" button (`F5-33`; the view itself is SCR-F5-05).
- **accept-challenge-sheet / otp-at-accept** — the one-time verification sheet at Accept, only where the tenant has enabled it (`F5-44`, `MS9-10`).
- **accepted-confirmation** — the page's own confirmation state changes within seconds, states what has been accepted and what happens next (`F5-48`).
- **accept-revalidation-failed** — the server re-check fails honestly: superseded version, stale money, already won/lost, or challenge unsatisfied; states plainly what happened and what the customer should do (`F5-47`).
- **declined** — the customer says no; the decline is a respond action on this page (`MS9-10`, `F5-43`; reason capture and suppression are tenant-side).
- **negotiate-requested** — the third respond act on this page, beside Accept and decline (`MS9-10`'s "accept / ask a question / negotiate per F5"; `F5-21`'s respond scope names accept, negotiate, decline). **Trigger:** the customer taps the negotiate affordance — a respond action on this page exactly as **declined** is, present in the same respond set and not behind Ask a question. **Outcome:** an honest acknowledgement that the request reached the company and that somebody will act on it, carrying **no queue position, no approval step and no pending-approval state**, because `F5-50` says none of those exist; the page keeps rendering the current version until the rep's revised version arrives at the same URL, which is **newer-version-rendered** below. **What the control collects before the request is sent is not pinned — designer decides, note the decision** (see the annotation above). Whether the acknowledgement states a *time* is likewise not pinned: `F5-50` makes same-day the company's operating promise, while `F5-53` forbids this page claiming when for a question — the safe reading is to say what happens next without inventing a commitment, and to note the decision. The tenant-side answer — the rep applying the discount and re-sharing — is `modules/M06`'s and is not drawn here.
- **newer-version-rendered** — a newer version supersedes the one rendered, at the same URL (`F5-47`'s current-version re-check; the negotiation outcome of `F5-50` arrives as a revised version here — this state is the answer to **negotiate-requested**).
- **question-acknowledged** — the question's honest acknowledgement: it reached the company, somebody will call; no chat thread, no reply box (`F5-52`, `F5-53`).
- **callback-requested** — the request-a-call acknowledgement; the page says when to expect the call (`F5-54`).
- **slow-connection-text-first** — text before weight: every figure, label and disclosure readable before any heavy asset arrives (`F5-07`, `F5-39`).
- **copy-link-confirmed** — working copy-link feedback (`MS9-14`).
- **qr-failed** — the scannable QR fails visibly rather than silently (`MS9-14`).
- **image-loading / image-present / image-missing** — the three distinct image states with a stable footprint (`MS9-23`).

## Data volume

Design at the Definition of Done's realistic volume, not demo volume (`F7-43`): one recommended system by default (`F5-35`) with variants present only where the designer added them; the full figure set of `F5-32` (system size, annual generation, monthly savings, total price, incentive, payable, payback); money summary, system summary, energy story, shadow study and equipment list all populated (`MS9-29`) — at C&I scale that means the equipment list and captures of a large design (the DoD's 221-panel design); several images each cycling loading/present/missing (`MS9-23`); financing options as labelled projections (`F5-42`); every figure with its persistent label beside it (`F5-36`) surviving the small viewport.
