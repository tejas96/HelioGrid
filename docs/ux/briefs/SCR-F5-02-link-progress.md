# SCR-F5-02 · Customer Link — Progress

Phase-2 of the same URL: done / current / waiting stages with dates, every wait attributed, plus the payment instrument, receipts and pre-installation facts.

**Module:** F5 (Customer link — the no-login tokenised customer journey) · **Personas:** Customer (an audience, never a role — no login, no account, no app) · **Context of use:** phone, returned to as a bookmark repeatedly during a long external wait; the page exists to kill silence, so it must answer "what is happening?" in one glance; slow connections are normal (text before weight); the payment moment is the highest-anxiety moment of the whole journey.

## Entry & exit

Reached from: the same URL as the proposal phase — the link advances in place when the deal is won; no re-issue, no new share, the customer's existing bookmark (`F5-61`, lifecycle law `F5-19`). Leads to: the tenant's own payment instrument for the due tranche (`F5-58`); at the terminal handed-over stage, the same URL becomes the Handover Pack (SCR-F5-03, `F5-70`); a link that cannot serve routes to the Link Failure Page (SCR-F5-04, `F5-25`). No other exit is pinned by the PRD — designer decides, note the decision.

### From `docs/prd/foundations/F5-customer-link.md`

- **F5-07** (P0) — **The page is mobile-first, readable in one sitting, and usable on a slow connection — text before weight.** The reading order puts what decides the question first; nothing load-bearing waits on a heavy asset; and no state of the page is reachable only after a large download. - **F5-55** (P0) — **A named person, with a phone number, is on the page in every phase.** This is one of the three things the source says carries a customer through their most anxious moment, and it is also what the final step of the journey requires the handover to leave behind: *"the handover should leave them knowing exactly who to call."* The page never shows a generic company line where a named person exists.
- **F5-57** (P0) — **This is the highest-anxiety moment in the entire journey, and the page answers it with exactly three things: an instant receipt, a named person to contact, and a clear statement of what happens next and when.** The source names all three; this document renders all three, and a payment surface that carries only the first is incomplete. - **F5-59** (P0) — **The receipt appears the moment the money is confirmed or recorded, and carries its confirmation state honestly.** A payment the tenant's account has confirmed and a payment a person recorded by hand are both receipts and are not presented as identical certainties; the state travels with the receipt onto this page exactly as the money module published it (`M11-41`, `M11-42`). This page never computes, re-rounds or re-labels a money figure (`F8-24`), and a figure that cannot be reconciled at display time renders provisional rather than final (`F8-12`). _(non-UI half, build-side: renders confirmation states exactly as M11 published; never computes, re-rounds or re-labels money; unreconciled renders provisional — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **F5-61** (P0) — **After the deal is won the same URL becomes the progress tracker, and it is the single highest-value surface in the delivery stage.** It shows what is done, what is happening now, and what is waiting — with dates. ## States

Three base states, then every screen-specific state from the slice and the rows:

- **loading** — the page reaches its decisive content (the stage answer) before any heavy asset (`F5-07`).
- **empty** — no stage row renders permanently empty: a stage the market skips is absent rather than an empty row (`F5-62`); a missing fact shortens the sentence rather than being invented.
- **error** — a link that cannot serve routes to the honest failure rendering (SCR-F5-04, `F5-25`).
- **normal** — done / current / waiting stages with dates (`F5-61`), canonical chain with market-pack labels (`F5-62`), named person with number (`F5-55`).
- **waiting-blocker-attributed** — a waiting stage naming the party, the reason, the date the wait started, and the typical duration where the pack declares one (`F5-63`).
- **expected-date-unknown** — where an expected-until date is unknown the page says the date is not yet known; it never computes a plausible one (per `F5-63`'s composes-nothing-unpublished law; no completion percentage exists on this page).
- **payment-due** — the due tranche's label and amount with the tenant's own payment instrument (`F5-58`, `M11-55`).
- **no-payment-instrument** — tenant has connected no account: no instrument shown, none fabricated; the page still shows what is due, the named contact and what happens next (`F5-58`).
- **receipt-pending-confirmation / awaiting-confirmation** — a payment recorded but not yet confirmed renders with its honest confirmation state — provisional, never final, never nothing (`F5-59`).
- **receipt-confirmed / receipt-present** — the receipt the instant money confirms, with its confirmation state exactly as M11 published it (`F5-59`, `M11-41`, `M11-45`).
- **installation-scheduled** — the four pre-arrival facts on the page: who is coming, when, how long it will take, and what disturbance to expect (`F5-68`). The page carries all four whichever way the evening-before crew message travelled — **sent automatically from the tenant's connected transactional channel** where one exists, or **composed for a person to send** where none is connected (the one path that claims no delivery) — so a customer who never saw the message still has every fact (`F5-68` as amended by owner ruling 2026-08-06 Q46, `M03-03`). The two send paths produce **no difference on this page**: there is no "message sent" badge, no delivery tick and no fallback variant of this state to draw — the ruling settles how the message travels, not what the page shows. **Send time (owner rulings 2026-08-06, Q50, Q54 and Q58) — build-side, and equally no difference on this page:** the message goes at the market pack's declared hour (a default, early evening) **read on the tenant's — the EPC's — timezone (`F1-10`, Q54), never the customer's**, inside the market's statutory messaging window (a floor) **evaluated on that same EPC clock (`F1-10`, Q58) — one clock for the hour and the window, never two**; a slot outside lawful hours sends at the last lawful moment before it, never after; a tenant may narrow the window and never widen it, and no setting exists on any surface — including this one — that moves a send past the floor (`F1-15`, `F1-17`). Where a customer sits in a different timezone from the EPC, the message still goes at the EPC's evening hour and is judged against the EPC's lawful hours — the rulings' knowingly accepted consequence, on the premise that a solar EPC's customers are local to it. *(The hour's clock clause was added by the pass applying Q54; this state previously read "a default, early evening in the customer's market timezone", the Q50 wording. The window's clock clause was added by the pass applying Q58, which this state had named no clock for. The hour and the window are both still undeclared for IN — register `Q53` is open — and this state names no time.)* **Its text is the registry's seeded `crew_arrival` template** (`F6-26`, owner ruling 2026-08-06 Q49) — the four facts plus the crew lead's name and number, in the reader's language; that key is named in `F6-26`'s own list, which owner ruling 2026-08-06 Q55 makes the registry's whole seeded key set. So this state has **no send-time variant to draw either**: no scheduled-for time, no countdown, no "sent at" line and no last-lawful-moment annotation appears on the page; the four facts are what the page owes and they are on it from the moment the installation is scheduled, whether or not the evening-before send has happened yet.
- **arrival-window-shown** — "your technician is on the way, arriving within the hour", derived from the assigned visit rather than from a live position (`M09-34`, P2/REC).
- **deal-cancelled-honest-state** — a cancelled deal shows its honest state rather than a fabricated stage or a dead page; the link is not silently killed (F5 lifecycle law; the dead-page rendering itself is SCR-F5-04).
- **question-acknowledged** — the question's honest acknowledgement: received, somebody will call, no chat thread (`F5-52`, `F5-53`).
- **callback-requested** — the request-a-call acknowledgement; the page says which lawful moment the call will come (`F5-54`).
- **never-gated-over-money** — an invariant across all states: an unpaid tranche and any tenant billing state change nothing on this page; no section hidden, dimmed, gated or annotated (`M11-55`'s never-gates-the-page fact).
- **slow-connection-text-first** — text before weight; the stage answer readable before any asset (`F5-07`).

## Data volume

Design at realistic volume, not demo volume (`F7-43`): the full canonical stage chain rendered with market-pack labels — done items with dates, one current item, one waiting item carrying all four wait facts (`F5-61`–`F5-63`); a multi-tranche payment schedule with several receipts, each carrying amount, tranche, date, mode, reference, attached file where one exists, and confirmation state (`M11-41`); mixed receipt states on one page (confirmed alongside hand-recorded/provisional, `F5-59`); the four pre-installation facts (`F5-68`); the named contact block and question/callback affordances present throughout (`F5-52`, `F5-55`).
