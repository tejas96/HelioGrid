# SCR-MS-04 · Studio Step 1 — Site Setup

Turn a lead into a designable site: project info, market-scoped parameters, confirmed location with honest solar data.

**Module:** MS (Design Studio) · **Personas:** Design Engineer, Sales Executive, Survey Engineer, EPC Owner · **Context of use:** Design Engineer is the primary author at a desk; Sales Executive and Survey Engineer may run remote-survey-derived setup (F2.M04.run-remote-survey) — often on a phone. Web primary authoring; mobile 375 px full parity (F7-30); touch behaviors per the S1-5a ruling and F7-32. Read access per lead visibility (F2). The canvas is light like every other surface — v1 is light-only, with no dark theme and no dark variant of any surface (`foundations/F7`, `F7-04`, P0; the global law sheet `docs/ux/claude-design-context.md` carries it for every session).

## Entry & exit

Reached from: step 1 of the studio wizard (SCR-MS-03) — the first step a new design opens on; a resumed design opens at its saved step (shell contract). Wizard-step gate (flagship note): Step 1 is the entry step — no prior gate admits it; its own Next gate is the first in the shell's defined order, **setup completeness** (MS12-03, shell contract). Leads to: Step 2 — Roof (SCR-MS-05), which consumes the confirmed location, calibration and canvas contract (MS1 forward contract).

## Requirements (verbatim)

### From `docs/prd/modules/M05-design-studio.md`

- **M05-14** (P0) — **The step pre-fills from the survey hand-off and everything is editable.** Address, location, site details and captured values arrive from the submitted survey (`M04-63`) with their provenance tiers intact, and the designer sees what the survey knows *and does not know* with equal prominence (`M04-64`): flagged gaps, skipped items, access constraints, structural observations. An edit that changes a value's provenance shows the change before it commits.
- **M05-15** (P0) — **The setup form carries the census field set, market-neutrally keyed.** Project name; customer name and phone; country/market and region; **utility** (the market pack's own label — sets the default tariff from pack data); site type Residential/Commercial; connection Single/Three phase; sanctioned load (kW); electricity tariff per kWh (auto-filled from the utility default, editable); average monthly bill (feeds the Step-4 size suggestion); company logo upload (max 5 MB, PNG/JPG — tenant branding per `M01`'s rules); ground-mount / open-access as a normal option. Currency and tariff data come from the tenant's market pack; no market's names appear in this module's body.
- **M05-16** (P0) — **Location: search with autocomplete or exact coordinates, then a touch-honest pin on the satellite canvas.** Pin placement is redesigned for touch (the v1 fixed-pin/drag-map-underneath pattern is the named failure); the designer still lands on the exact roof, with zoom. The imagery tile the design is traced on is **pinned at capture: it never changes underneath the design.** Tile fetch failure still opens the studio — blank canvas plus manual calibration (§M05.4) — it never blocks. _(non-UI half, build-side: imagery tile pinned at capture, never changes underneath; failure never blocks — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-17** (P0) — **Confirming the location produces the Solar Data card, and its figures obey the energy source-of-record ladder.** The card shows irradiance (kWh/m²/day), peak sun hours and a source label. The market's **energy source of record** supplies the figures where available — v1 reference implementation: PVGIS with its documented database ladder (SARAH3 → ERA5) — labelled per `F8-08`'s fixed copy; the built-in latitude-fit fallback is always labelled "±10%". The source is never switched silently (`F8-09`, cited). _(non-UI half, build-side: energy source-of-record ladder (PVGIS SARAH3→ERA5); ±10% fallback, never silent switch — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-18** (P0) — **Site Intelligence is an asynchronous enhancement with four honest states — loading / unavailable / unreachable / ok — and never a dependency.** In its "ok" state it shows max panels (~kWp), roof area, sunshine h/yr, roof faces, imagery date and quality (HIGH/MED/BASE). Every path it serves works without it; its absence is stated, not styled as an error. _(non-UI half, build-side: async building-insights-class provider; enhancement, never a dependency — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-19** (P0) — **Relocating the pin more than 25 m wipes the whole design — kept as a guard, made a clear, undoable confirmation.** The confirmation states exactly what will be lost (roofs, obstructions, panels — everything traced against the old tile); it is never a silent reset, and it is undoable. _(non-UI half, build-side: relocation >25m wipes all traced geometry; undoable — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-20** (P0) — **The sanctioned-load soft cap warns and never blocks.** Auto-design and manual design may exceed the sanctioned load captured by the survey (`M04-45`); doing so raises a warning naming the actual limit — *"this is a real approval blocker"* — because exceeding it jeopardises the market's approval process (the rule content is pack data). The design remains editable and savable; the warning travels to readiness (§M05.9) and the market compliance checklist (§M05.11). _(non-UI half, build-side: soft cap never blocks; warning travels to readiness and compliance checklist — for awareness, not for drawing)_

## States

Base: **loading** · **empty** · **error** (a Maps failure never dead-ends the step, MS1-15/MS1-17; data fetches never block, MS1-22; explicit states everywhere per the studio's no-blank-screen law).

Screen-specific:

- **normal** — setup form + location canvas.
- **prefilled-from-survey** — survey pre-fills location (arrives CONFIRMED at the surveyed pin) and site details, provenance hints "from survey, {date}" (MS1-01, M05-14).
- **prefilled-from-lead** — customer name, phone, site type pre-filled from the lead/customer record, hint "from lead" (MS1-01).
- **first-run-walkthrough** — coach marks over the live screen (search → confirm → next), shown once, dismissable forever, reachable from Help (MS1-08).
- **phone-invalid** — inline corrective error per the market's pack.formats rule; other fields stay usable (MS1-02).
- **utility-disabled-until-region** — utility select disabled with state-aware placeholder until a region is chosen (MS1-04).
- **logo-upload-error** — override upload violation (size/type) shows a clear error, no write (MS1-12).
- **map-empty** — map empty state keeps the typed-coordinates path alive (MS1-15).
- **maps-sdk-failed** — "Couldn't load the map — Retry"; retry re-attempts in place, never memoized for the session (MS1-17, MS1-15).
- **pin-pending** — a pending pin; Confirm Location enabled only now (MS1-18, MS1-16).
- **location-confirmed** — address/latLng/confirmed stored with built-in irradiance and "±10% — verify" provenance until real data lands (MS1-18).
- **relocation-wipe-confirm / pin-move-wipe-confirm** — the pre-wipe dialog names what will be cleared with counts ("1 roof, 24 panels, layouts…"); Cancel keeps everything; confirm is still undoable (MS1-20, M05-19).
- **solar-loading** — Solar Data figures pending post-confirm fetch (MS1-22, M05-17).
- **solar-no-coverage** — the source of record has no coverage; stated plainly (M05-17, MS1-22).
- **solar-unreachable** — fetch failed; retryable, never a silent switch (M05-17).
- **solar-ok** — irradiance (kWh/m²/day), peak sun hours, source label per the source-of-record ladder (M05-17).
- **energy-fallback-labelled** — the built-in latitude-fit fallback always labelled "±10%" (M05-17, MS1-18).
- **tile-fetch-failed-blank-canvas** — tile failure still opens the studio: blank canvas plus manual calibration; never blocks (M05-16).
- **site-intel-loading / site-intel-unavailable / site-intel-unreachable / site-intel-ok** — the four honest Site Intelligence states; unavailable/unreachable in plain language ("manual design mode"); ok shows provider badge, imagery-quality badge, stats grid, honest footer (M05-18, MS1-23).
- **sanctioned-load-warning** — exceeding sanctioned load warns with the actual limit ("this is a real approval blocker") and never blocks (M05-20).
- **coming-soon-option** — carried from the Screens Register slice; not pinned to a PRD row in this brief (note: no tier gating or lock copy may exist on any option per MS1-09's Q28 law) — designer confirms the anchor and notes the decision.

## Data volume

A single-project form over pack-scale pickers: the India pack ships 37 states/UTs and per-state DISCOM lists with generic fallback (MS1-03) — region and utility selects must be designed at that volume. One satellite viewport with one pin; the Site Intelligence stats grid holds up to five figures each independently hideable when absent (MS1-23). Logo preview at max 70×200 px within a 5 MB PNG/JPG constraint (MS1-12).

## Numbers carrying provenance

Each user-visible number carries its F8 provenance tier in the design: **electricity tariff per kWh** (auto-filled from pack, "representative rate; edit to match your actual bill" — manual wins, MS1-05); **average monthly bill** (nullable-not-zero, MS1-06); **sanctioned load (kW)** and the warning's **actual limit** (MS1-10, M05-20); **coordinates at 5 dp** (MS1-14); **irradiance (kWh/m²/day)** and **peak sun hours** with source label — real source vs "±10%" fallback (M05-17, MS1-18, MS1-22); Site Intelligence **max panels (~kWp)**, **roof area (m/ft preference)**, **sunshine h/yr**, **roof-face count**, **imagery date** ("date unknown" when absent) (M05-18, MS1-23); the wipe dialog's **counts** ("1 roof, 24 panels…") and the **25 m** relocation tolerance (MS1-20, M05-19); pre-fill provenance hints **"from survey, {date}" / "from lead"** (MS1-01); logo limits **5 MB / 70×200 px** (MS1-12).
