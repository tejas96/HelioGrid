# SCR-MS-07 · Studio Step 4 — Components

Choose panel, target capacity, inverter/topology/MLPE and battery via resolved-catalog pickers, plus the 11-column compare decision matrix.

**Module:** MS (Design Studio) · **Personas:** Design Engineer, Sales Executive, EPC Owner · **Context of use:** Design Engineer authors; Sales Executive does compare-led selling (MS4 §2) — the compare matrix is a decision surface used in front of customers. Web + mobile parity (F7-30). Catalog management stays elsewhere (Owner+Operations) — this step only consumes the resolved catalog.

## Entry & exit

Reached from: Step 4 of the wizard (SCR-MS-03), after Step 3. Wizard-step gate (flagship note): the shell's **at least one roof** gate admits the user this far; this step's own Next gate is the shell's **panel/inverter/capacity** gate, and the step's gate messages block in order with plain reasons — "Select a panel to continue" → "Select an inverter to continue" → "Set a target capacity to continue" (MS4-03, MS12-03 shell contract). Leads to: the **Panel layout** step (SCR-MS-08) — named here by step name and screen id, with **no visible "step 5"**: auto-placement is folded into Panel layout and **no user-visible "step 5" ever exists**, so nothing on this screen's forward affordance may label or number one; step position is the shell's step indicator ("n / 9") and the shell's alone (M05-02, MS12-01, shell contract on SCR-MS-03). The chosen module/target flows to the layout fill, inverter/topology/MLPE to SLD, catalog + pricebook to BOM (MS4 forward contract).

## Requirements (verbatim)

### From `docs/prd/modules/M05-design-studio.md`

- **M05-37** (P0) — **The shared component-picker pattern (defined once, here):** an accordion of sections — **Panel → Capacity → Inverter → Battery** — each showing its done-state and current choice, reopenable in any order after first completion. Every component section offers **three entry paths: Browse database · Upload datasheet (PDF extraction) · Enter specs manually.** Browsing searches the tenant's resolved catalog (`M01-32`/`M01-33`/`M01-38`, consumed); pickers badge **scheme-keyed certification compliance** per the tenant market's declared schemes (`M01-34`/`F1-19`, consumed — the IN pack's two schemes are `F1-44`'s data); a missing product is added in-flow by `M01-39`'s **three add paths — single-product form · datasheet PDF extraction · spreadsheet upload** (`M01-39`/`M01-40`/`M01-41`, consumed), with extraction always reviewed before commit. *(Final review: the entry triad and the add triad are distinct — browsing finds a product, it never adds one; `M01-39` is the authority and `M06-28` carries the same reading.)* The census marked the manual and datasheet routes "available-later"; DD12 rules all three paths present — the tension with the no-later-buckets scope law is recorded, not resolved (traceability). The **Battery** section appears in DD12's owner-supplied v1 screenshots and not in the census — recorded here, not resolved; the census gains nothing and loses nothing by it.
- **M05-38** (P0) — **Panel section carries the census surface:** selected-panel summary (brand, model, dimensions, Voc/Vmp/Isc, wattage large); search with result count; filters — min/max watt; technology (All Types · Mono PERC · TOPCon · Bifacial · Poly · HJT); per-scheme certification on/off filters as declared by the market pack; list rows with brand mark, brand + model, "watt · technology · dimensions" and scheme badges.
- **M05-39** (P0) — **Capacity section:** target capacity in kWp (0.1 steps); **Auto** = maximum fit after setbacks and obstructions (unavailable until a panel is chosen and a roof exists, reason shown); site-intelligence cross-check line (info only, never a dependency); **bill-based suggestion** ("From your {bill}/month we recommend ~Y kWp", tap applies — bill from Step 1); Continue unavailable while capacity is 0. _(non-UI half, build-side: auto computes maximum fit after setbacks and obstructions — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-40** (P0) — **Inverter section:** recommended line ("{brand} {model} ({kW}×{count}, {phase}) → DC/AC ratio X", tap applies); selected summary (brand, model, #MPPTs, voltage range, phases, efficiency, kW large); count 1–10; **live DC/AC ratio health — >1.35 high (clipping risk), <0.90 low (oversized), 0.90–1.35 healthy**; search + count + list (kW · phase · MPPT count · voltage range, recommended badge). After choice: **DC collection topology** (String inverters / Central + combiners) and **MLPE** (None / DC optimisers) — MLPE components come from the catalog (`M01-45`); the absence of an MLPE electrical model is this module's recorded non-goal (§5). _(non-UI half, build-side: recommendation and live DC/AC ratio computation with census thresholds — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-41** (P0) — **Compare options is honest about its basis and applies atomically.** Unavailable with no roof (reason shown). It states its basis: target vs max fill, source-of-record vs built-in energy estimate, catalog version, warnings. Columns per the census: Option (RECOMMENDED/CURRENT markers, availability/certification notes) · Inverter (+ DC/AC) · Fits (panels × watt → kWp) · Module efficiency · Annual generation · Net cost (subsidy separate) · Payback · 25-year savings (+ ROI%) · Warranty · Install (complexity + array weight) · Apply/selected/feasibility. Decision cards (topic, choice, reason, inputs) and a fixed-assumptions footnote (tariff escalation, horizon, margin, subsidy scheme — the scheme is pack data). Apply sets panel + inverter + count in **one undoable action**; if panels are already placed with a different panel, a replace-panel confirmation intervenes. _(non-UI half, build-side: apply is one atomic undoable action; basis honesty stated before columns — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M05-43** (P0) — **Catalog state is honest in the picker: out-of-stock or discontinued items are flagged, and existing outputs keep their original pricing.** A panel that goes unavailable after selection flags in the design; sent proposals keep the rate version they were built with (`M01-43`/`M01-44`, consumed; the proposal-side guarantee is `modules/M06`'s). Archived items never break existing references (`M01-42`, consumed). _(non-UI half, build-side: sent proposals keep rate version; archived items never break references — for awareness, not for drawing)_

## States

Base: **loading** · **empty** · **error** (studio no-blank-screen law; catalog reads and mutations per shell contract).

Screen-specific:

- **panel-section-open / capacity-section-open / inverter-section-open / battery-section-open** — one accordion section open at a time, initial = first incomplete (MS4-01).
- **section-done / section-done-summary** — done-state summary on each collapsed header: "{brand} {watt}W", "{n} kWp", "{brand} {acKw}kW", "{kWh} kWh / none" (MS4-01, MS4-02).
- **gated-with-reason** — step gate messages block in order with plain reasons (MS4-03).
- **capacity-zero-continue-blocked** — Continue unavailable while capacity is 0 / until set (M05-39, MS4-14).
- **filter-no-match** — no-match state explains and offers Clear filters (MS4-11, MS4-10).
- **swap-guard-dialog / replace-panel-confirm** — placed-panel guard: what changes · module count · keep-or-refill; one undo step; identical on picker and Compare→Apply paths (MS4-12, MS4-33, M05-41).
- **datasheet-review / datasheet-extraction-review** — datasheet PDF extraction always reviewed before commit (MS4-06, M05-37).
- **auto-unavailable-reason** — Auto states the reason: "draw a roof first" / "choose a panel first" (MS4-15, M05-39).
- **no-fit-explained** — NO-FIT: the reason, the nearest fits, the practical suggestions (multi-unit, phase change) (MS4-19).
- **compare-sheet-open / compare-open** — full-screen dialog, focus trap, Esc; BASIS statement, warnings, 11 columns, decision cards, fixed-assumptions footnote (MS4-27, MS4-28, MS4-30, MS4-32, M05-41).
- **compare-disabled-no-roof / compare-unavailable-no-roof** — Compare disabled with a stated reason when no roof exists (MS4-27, M05-41).
- **recomputing** — after Apply the sheet stays open and re-computes so the choice can be verified against alternatives (MS4-33).
- **item-flagged-unavailable** — out-of-stock/discontinued flagged; a selected panel that goes unavailable flags in the design; availability travels everywhere the product appears (M05-43, MS4-08).
- **no-scheme-chrome** — certification flags are scheme-keyed pack data; a market that declares no schemes renders no scheme badges/filters (MS4-09).

## Data volume

Design at a tenant's real resolved catalog behind every picker (panels, inverters, batteries) — searchable lists with result counts, filters and badges (MS4-10, MS4-11); the compare matrix at its capped certification-first shortlist plus the current selection, each row computing 11 columns (MS4-29, MS4-30); the swap guard at a fully placed design (a 221-panel design is the PRD's flagship scale reference — the dialog states the module count, MS4-12).

## Numbers carrying provenance

Each user-visible number carries its F8 provenance tier in the design: panel **wattage, dimensions, Voc/Vmp/Isc** (M05-38); **target capacity kWp** (0.1 quantised, MS4-14); **Auto max-fit kWp** (M05-39, MS4-15); the **bill-based suggestion** "From your ₹X/month bill → ~Y kWp" (MS4-16, M05-39); the cross-check **panels and equivalent kWp** with the "your module differs" caveat (MS4-17); **DC/AC ratio** with its health bands >1.35 / <0.90 / 0.90–1.35 and the recommendation's **unit, count, phases, resulting ratio** (M05-40, MS4-18); inverter **count 1–10** with stated min/max (M05-40, MS4-21); battery **usable kWh, voltage window, warranty, price, quantity** (MS4-24); compare columns — **fits (panels × watt → kWp), module efficiency, annual kWh (unshaded basis, stated), net cost with subsidy sub-line, payback, 25-yr savings + ROI %, warranty, array weight** (MS4-30, M05-41); the **fixed-assumptions footnote** values (escalation, horizon, margin, subsidy basis) (MS4-32, M05-41); per-entry **provenance and effective-from** (MS4-07); every zero/warn explained by the note taxonomy (MS4-31).
