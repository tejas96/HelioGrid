## 2. PRICING NORMS

**India vs global — two different pricing paradigms:**

- **Global (US/AU/EU) = per-seat/month, USD.** Aurora $159–259/user/mo, HelioScope $99–300/user/mo, SolarPlus ~$135–520/mo, PVcase $990–30,000/yr. Effective cost per designer/month runs **$100–$300+**.
- **India = per-organization or per-capacity, INR, ~3–5x cheaper.** ARKA ₹7,500–16,500/mo (≈**$90–$200/mo for the whole org**, not per seat) with project caps; Reslink $949–1,899/yr (≈**$80–$160/mo**) tiered by kW/MW capacity. Indian tools deliberately avoid per-seat pricing because EPCs employ many low-cost designers and are highly price-sensitive; capacity- or project-cap tiers align cost to revenue.
- **Per-design micro-pricing** (Pylon: $4–$10/design) is an emerging middle path — no seat lock-in, pay only when you produce a proposal. Attractive for low-volume/seasonal Indian installers.
- **Annual discounting is aggressive:** ARKA "up to 50%," Reslink "up to 52%" vs monthly — a working-capital lever for young Indian EPCs.

**Freemium / OpenSolar's monetization (the key disruptor to understand):**
OpenSolar charges installers **nothing** and instead monetizes the transaction layer:
1. **Partner-funded distribution** — hardware distributors and equipment lenders pay for **preferred placement** and access to the 28,000-installer network.
2. **Finance referral / CashFlow payments** — its solar-specific payments system takes a cut of financed/paid transactions.
3. **Bespoke enterprise software** — paid custom builds for manufacturers and solar-finance companies serving their dealer networks.
Result: $11.8M revenue on a "free" product (2025). This is the model an India entrant must either beat on localization or copy on the finance/hardware-referral side (e.g., PSU/NBFC loan referral, ALMM-approved panel distributor placement).

## 3. FEATURE GAPS A NEW INDIA ENTRANT CAN EXPLOIT

Market context that creates the opening: **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** targets **1 crore (10M) households / 27–30 GW residential rooftop by FY2026-27**, ₹75,021 cr (~$8.5B) outlay, up to **40% subsidy** (₹33k for 1kW, ₹78k for 2kW, ₹85k+ for 3kW). Only ~24 lakh households done by Dec 2025 (~24% of target) — a huge, under-digitized funnel. Reslink's own framing pegs the India solar-software TAM conservatively at **$50M+**, and notes most EPCs still run on **"Excel and WhatsApp."**

Exploitable gaps:

1. **PM Surya Ghar subsidy automation** — Today ARKA and OpenSolar require the EPC to **manually configure subsidy slabs** before sales can quote. A tool that auto-computes net-of-subsidy price by state × DISCOM × system size (with live slab updates) is a clear wedge.
2. **DISCOM interconnection & paperwork** — Net-metering rules, feasibility applications, load-sanction, and inspection workflows vary state-by-state and are almost entirely manual. Auto-generated, DISCOM-specific application packets are unaddressed by incumbents.
3. **ALMM / DCR compliance checking** — Only Reslink explicitly does this. Auto-validating BOM against the ALMM approved-models list (and DCR content rules) at design time prevents costly rework.
4. **WhatsApp-first sales** — The dominant channel in India; no incumbent has a native WhatsApp proposal-send / lead-capture / customer-approval loop. Voice/vernacular WhatsApp bots for lead qualification are greenfield.
5. **Voice agents + vernacular UI** — Hindi + regional-language design/proposal flows and voice-driven site capture for field sales agents (who are not English-first). No incumbent offers this.
6. **BOM + structural procurement automation** — Structure is 8–15% of project cost yet BOM/structural sizing is mostly manual and trapped with a "key person." Auto-BOM straight from the 3D model into procurement is under-served (Reslink and ARKA partially address; room to go deeper into vendor price feeds).
7. **Handoff loss across sales→design→costing→proposal** — Cited as the #1 EPC pain point; a single mobile-first record that eliminates re-keying between teams is a differentiator.
8. **Mobile-first, on-site, offline** — Field teams demand phone-based design in minutes (Reslink's core bet); most global tools are desktop-heavy.
9. **India-specific financing integration** — Embedded PSU-bank / NBFC loan eligibility and application (the OpenSolar-CashFlow analog for India) is open.
