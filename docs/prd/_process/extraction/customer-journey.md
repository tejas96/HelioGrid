## 2 · The 13 steps (C1–C13)

| key | content | target_doc |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **They make an enquiry** (L971–983). CUSTOMER: trigger is "they call, or a rep adds them"; what they experience is a phone call. They are shopping and "have probably contacted two or three companies the same week". EPC SIDE: "**Speed of first response is the single biggest predictor of who wins the job.**" Response-time signals as written: called back within an hour → strong signal; called back in three days → "the job is already lost"; called by three different people from the same company → looks disorganised, "*this is what the duplicate check at Stage 2 prevents*". POST-OVERLAY: no docs/15 ruling amends C1; D13 (lead sources v1 = manual, CSV, inbound voice) is HONORED, website/WhatsApp stay "later" cards. | F5 (+M02) |
| C2 | **First conversation** (L985–995). CUSTOMER: a rep asking about their electricity bill, roof and timeline; what they are silently judging is "does this person know more than I do?" EPC SIDE: "*This is why the agent's business-knowledge base matters — the same answers must be right whoever, or whatever, is speaking.*" THEY RECEIVE: "a WhatsApp confirming the site visit — date, time, name of who is coming, and the person's phone number" (four mandatory fields). POST-OVERLAY: D24 is PARTIAL — the guided config + KB + unanswered loop are honored, but its "locked by platform" half is superseded by D36 (all config tenant-editable); R10 — corrections never auto-train, the KB changes only by explicit owner promotion. D32 — the confirmation message is composed by the product and pasted by the rep, not sent by the app. | F5 (+M07, +M02) |
| C3 | **The site visit — which may not happen yet (D30)** (L997–1012). CUSTOMER, remote survey: "the customer experiences *nothing here*" and gets their proposal the same day instead of next week — "That speed is often what wins the job"; the visit then happens after they show interest, to verify before installation. CUSTOMER, physical survey: "somebody on their roof for 30–45 minutes with a phone". TRUST RULE: what builds it is "the surveyor explaining what they are photographing and why"; what destroys it is "silent photographing, then leaving without saying what happens next". THEY RECEIVE: a WhatsApp — "*Survey done. Your proposal will reach you by Thursday.*" — "**A promise with a date.**" POST-OVERLAY: D30 HONORED (two modes, remote-first residential; the provenance split rides R18); D35 HONORED (survey photos are reference, not measurement — no LiDAR/AR/auto-measure). | F5 (+M04) |
| C4 | **The wait** (L1014–1019). CUSTOMER: "silence, for 1–3 days." EPC SIDE: "This is where enthusiasm decays and competitors land their proposals first. **Nothing in the product should let this gap be silent** — an automatic 'we are working on your design' message on day two costs nothing and holds the position." POST-OVERLAY CONTRADICTION (recorded, not resolved): D32 is HONORED — the app does not send on the customer's behalf in v1, and journey § CROSS-CUTTING scopes notifications to push + in-app (staff surfaces). An **automatic** day-two customer message therefore has no send channel at launch; the v1-honest form is a product-generated draft the rep pastes, or a staff-side nudge task. Flag for `open-questions.md`. | F5 (+F6, +M06) |
| C5 | **The proposal arrives** (L1021–1043). CUSTOMER: "a WhatsApp with a message, a PDF, and a link"; "the moment of maximum attention. They will open it once, properly, probably in the evening, probably on a phone, possibly with their spouse." WHAT THE LINK SHOWS: system size, annual generation, monthly savings · total price, subsidy, what they actually pay · payback period · "**A 3D view of their own roof** — this is the moment that separates you from a PDF emailed by a competitor" · financing options · **Accept** · **Ask a question**. HONESTY RULE: a Path B (no design) proposal says so on the document — "*Indicative proposal. A site survey and shadow analysis will confirm the final figures.*" — because "Competitors print estimates as certainties. When a customer compares three proposals and only yours admits which numbers are estimated, that reads as confidence, not weakness." POST-OVERLAY: R1 — the customer-facing document and link say "**Proposal**" in all three locales; "quote"/"quotation" are banned from UI strings (search aliases only). R18 — the disclaimer wording is driven by the provenance enum (measured/derived/estimated/assumed), no fifth tier. R5 — energy figures carry "Real · PVGIS ({database})" or "Built-in estimate ±10%", and never silently switch source. R17 — OPEX/PPA is a proposal document type only; the honesty label on financial projections is the only downstream branch. D32 — rep pastes PDF + link manually; opens are tracked, delivery is not. R6 — Accept is now OTP-challenged above the tenant threshold (C.lifecycle.4). | F5 (+M06, +F8) |
| C6 | **They think about it** (L1045–1060). CUSTOMER: "comparing quotes, asking a relative who 'knows about solar', searching the panel brand online, worrying about the roof leaking." "**Nothing happens in the app. Everything happens in their head.** Typical decision window: 3 days to 3 weeks." Their real questions "are rarely about price": will this actually reduce my bill · what if it does not work · who fixes it if something breaks in year four · will it damage my roof · is the subsidy real, and who does that paperwork. EPC SIDE: "**This is exactly what the voice agent must be able to answer** (D24 knowledge base). Not a sales pitch — the boring, specific reassurance a person needs before spending ₹8 lakh." POST-OVERLAY: D24 PARTIAL (config half tenant-owned under D36); R10 — an unanswered question becomes a review-queue item the owner promotes in one tap; nothing auto-trains. NOTE: the source lists no "goes wrong" items for C6. | F5 (+M07) |
| C7 | **The follow-up** (L1062–1077). CUSTOMER: "a call. Sometimes from the rep, sometimes from the agent." The agent "opens by saying it is automated (the default; D36)"; handled well this is fine — "an agent that answers a warranty question at 8pm is more useful than a rep who calls back on Tuesday." SHIPPED DEFAULTS that prevent the bad version: "capped attempts, a 9am–9pm window, per-customer language, an always-offered human hand-off"; source says "The owner can change them and owns that choice (D36), so the defaults are set to protect the customer out of the box." POST-OVERLAY — D36 is PARTIAL and this is the amended half: config is fully tenant-owned, **but the statutory floor is enforced by our non-swappable `ComplianceGate`, not merely surfaced**; the gate's MECHANISM is non-swappable while its RULESET is per-market pack data (IN = TRAI/DLT: DND scrub, 9am–9pm, DLT series, opt-out, recording retention). SUPERSEDED: the source's blanket "the owner can change any of it" no longer holds for the statutory items — "Tenants configure within the law, not around it." A market with no voice ruleset cannot enable outbound voice. R3 — agent languages (6) stay independent of UI languages (3). | F5 (+M07, +F1) |
| C8 | **The decision** (L1079–1087). CUSTOMER: "tapping **Accept** on the link, or going quiet, or saying no." ACCEPT → "they expect immediate acknowledgement. A WhatsApp within seconds, not a silence that makes them wonder if the tap registered." NEGOTIATE → "they ask for a discount. The rep can apply it and reshare the same day — there is no approval hop (D34). **They should not wait two days for an answer**, and now nothing in the product makes them." NO → "the reason is recorded (Stage 7). They should not then be called for six months." POST-OVERLAY: R6/D33 — Accept above a tenant-set value threshold (tenant currency) is OTP-challenged, and the acceptance record captures link id, contact, OTP verification, IP and user agent. D34 HONORED — no approval flow; only the arithmetic guard (payable ≤ ₹0 blocks Generate, below-cost warns), checked at Generate (R12). R9 — Lost/Disqualified reasons are mandatory (6 reasons); "not interested" suppresses the no-call task for 6 months; "postponed" auto-resurfaces on the given date at 09:00 tenant-local. D10 — actual acceptance happens only by tapping Accept on the link, never by a verbal agent agreement. CONTRADICTION (recorded): "a WhatsApp within seconds" is unsendable under D32 in v1 — the acknowledgement channel at launch is the link's own confirmation state plus a rep-pasted message. | F5 (+M06, +M07) |
| C9 | **Paying the advance** (L1089–1094). CUSTOMER: "a payment link, then a receipt." "Highest-anxiety moment in the entire journey — first real money to a company they met three weeks ago." WHAT REDUCES THE ANXIETY (the three requirements): "an instant receipt, a named person to contact, and a clear statement of what happens next and when." POST-OVERLAY: D38 is SUPERSEDED (owner override 2026-07-24, directive 4) — payments, subscriptions, billing, entitlements, usage tracking and gateway integration are IN v1. R4 — tenant customer-collections are BYO-Razorpay payment links per tenant behind `PaymentLinkPort`; "platform never touches tenant funds". Tranche schedule and receipt records sit on the money path. NOTE: the source lists no "goes wrong" items for C9. | F5 (+M11) |
| C10 | **The long wait — the most under-served part of solar** (L1096–1116). CUSTOMER: "weeks. Material ordering, scheduling, and **net metering approval, which alone can take 3–6 weeks with the DISCOM.**" "**This is where every Indian solar customer becomes unhappy**, and almost always because of silence rather than delay." EPC SIDE: "**The single highest-value screen in Stage 8 is the customer progress link** — the same URL the proposal used", showing done / current / waiting stages with dates, e.g. `✅ Advance received 12 Aug · ✅ Material ordered 14 Aug · 🔵 Installation scheduled 22 Aug · ⬜ Commissioning · ⬜ Net metering — waiting for DISCOM approval, Applied 15 Aug · typically 3–6 weeks`. "**That last line prevents more support calls than anything else in the product.** A delay you explained is tolerable; a delay you hid is a complaint." POST-OVERLAY: R2 — the customer-link stage display uses the canonical 9-stage chain (`WON → MATERIAL_ORDERED → DISPATCHED → INSTALLATION → ELECTRICAL_METERING → UTILITY_INSPECTION → COMMISSIONED → INCENTIVE_CLAIMED → HANDED_OVER`, + `CANCELLED`); enum names are market-neutral and the blocker party is `utility` (was `discom`), while "DISCOM inspection" / "Net metering" / "Subsidy claimed" are IN market-pack LABELS. Blocker sub-states (waiting on utility/customer/material/us) ride on any stage. CROSS-REF (journey L914, Stage 8): "**never block the customer's progress link over money** — chase the person, do not punish the view" — carried here as C.lifecycle.7. | F5 (+M08, +F1) |
| C11 | **Installation** (L1118–1126). CUSTOMER: "a crew on their roof for 1–2 days." What they want to know: "who is coming, when, how long, and will there be noise and mess. A WhatsApp the evening before with the crew lead's name and number covers it." EPC SIDE: "*The existing Installation Plan already derives the real work sequence from the structural model — foundation → legs → rafters → purlins → modules → stringing → BOS. It plugs in here rather than being rebuilt.*" POST-OVERLAY: R16 — there is no crew user in v1; the coordinator (Manager preset) runs the checklist, ticks are attributed to the coordinator, and an optional free-text "done by" captures the crew member's name; "Crew sees no money because crew sees no screen"; an Installer preset is v2. D32 — the evening-before message is rep-pasted. | F5 (+M08) |
| C12 | **Commissioning & handover** (L1128–1135). CUSTOMER: "the system switching on, and a pile of documents." THEY RECEIVE: "the handover pack — warranty documents, commissioning certificate, net-metering approval, and how to read their generation." REFERRAL RULE: "This is the moment they will decide whether to refer you. **Ask for the referral here**, while the roof is new and the first bill is about to drop — not six months later." POST-OVERLAY: R15 — the referral tag and the "came from" chip ship in v1 (CRM core), linking referrer customer → referred lead (source=referral), visible on both records and in win/loss analytics; **the credits ledger is the spec-locked exclusion** — no monetary credit, no redemption, no balance in v1. R2 — `HANDED_OVER` is the terminal stage. The link becomes the document pack here (C.lifecycle.6). | F5 (+M08, +M02) |
| C13 | **Living with it *(beyond v1)*** (L1137–1140). CUSTOMER: "They will want: generation monitoring, cleaning reminders, service contact, and eventually warranty claims. Out of scope for v1 (D9), but the handover should leave them knowing exactly who to call." POST-OVERLAY: D9 is PARTIAL — the scope half is HONORED (no inventory/PO/O&M), only its 5-state shorthand is dead (R2). R17 — a PPA billing/metering engine is an **explicit non-goal** recorded in docs/00, which also rules out meter ingestion and recurring monitoring in v1. The one live v1 requirement in this row is the handover leaving a named service contact. | F5 (§Non-goals) |
