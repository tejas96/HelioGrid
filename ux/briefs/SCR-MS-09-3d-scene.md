# SCR-MS-09 · Studio 3D Scene

Full-screen 3D inspection with simulated sun, shading truth, in-scene table/structure editing and capture.

**Module:** M05 · Design Studio · **Personas:** Design Engineer (author), Sales Executive, EPC Owner, Sales (read-only) · **Context of use:** one shared surface opened over the editor steps — a designer inspecting shading truth at a desk, a sales person walking a customer through the model on a phone; web primary with mobile full parity and the S5-3 visible 3D controls, so nothing is gesture- or keyboard-only; the customer sees this same surface read-only inside the proposal link (Q27). The scene is light like every other surface — v1 is light-only, with no dark theme and no dark variant of any surface (`foundations/F7`, `F7-04`, P0; the shell carries the same law, SCR-MS-03).

## Entry & exit

Not a wizard step of its own — **no wizard-step gate admits it**; it is a full-screen overlay over the steps that host it. Reached from: the layout editor — "3D is one tap away" (M05-50) and 3D entry from the layout (MS6-37); Step 3 — Obstructions, where "Open 3D replaces the step with the scene until closed, state intact" (MS3-11); per M05 §M05.8 behavior detail it is one shared surface reachable from roof, obstruction and layout steps and from the Done screen's share link — always the same model, never a fork. Leads to: back to the step it replaced (close → 2D, M05-51; state intact, MS3-11); capture mode feeds Step 7's proposal hero shots (MS6-37 → SCR-MS-10); the energy report trigger opens the report sheet over the scene (MS6-37, M05-54); the customer share rendering rides the proposal link per Q27 (MS6-37) — read-only.

## States

- loading (assets-loading — lazy per-type streaming: only the types the project actually contains are streamed, and a type still loading renders the procedural fallback rather than a hole in the scene, MS3-38)
- empty — the scene opened before any panel exists: reached from Step 3 it renders roofs and obstructions with no array (MS3-11), and 3D entry is disabled at 0 roofs so the scene is never opened onto nothing (MS2-01 on SCR-MS-05). The structure and per-panel cards have nothing to address; a card that cannot be shown explains why rather than rendering blank (see structure-card-unavailable-explains-why).
- error — a failure never blanks the scene and never fakes it: missing or corrupt assets render the procedural fallback and a 404 never crashes the scene (MS3-38); and because per-panel access values come from the HEADLESS engine and never from the renderer, so that the picture and the physics can never disagree (MS6-33), an access result that cannot be produced renders as unavailable rather than as a plausible tint.
- edit-save-failed — **in-scene edits are design mutations** — table settings, structure presets, profile, foundation, tilt/clearance, leg plan (M05-53, MS6-35, MS6-48) — and follow the studio's save law, failing fast with an honest message, never a silent no-op (M05-09, shell contract on SCR-MS-03); view aids (Show/Ghost/Hide, All/Isolate) are never saved and so are unaffected (M05-53). Capture mode records the shot; the capture-save failure contract, including its remedy, is Step 7's (M05-57, MS7-06 on SCR-MS-10).

## Data volume

A 221-panel design rendered whole: per-panel access values for every panel from the headless engine, shadows cast by roofs, parapets, obstructions, walkways, rails, arresters and wall inverters simultaneously, a 12-month heatmap track, structure members (legs, rafters, purlins, braces) drawn as real extruded sections for every table, and up to 11 obstruction types with per-type 3D models or procedural fallbacks. The energy report sheet carries 12 monthly bars, a multi-line loss breakdown and a 25-year projection over this same design.
