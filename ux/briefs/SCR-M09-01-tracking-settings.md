# SCR-M09-01 · Tracking Settings

Owner settings-class surface: per-employee tracking toggles with cost-stating confirmations, draft per-seat price, force-stop hour, tenant default fence radius.

**Module:** M09 · Field workforce · **Personas:** EPC Owner · **Context of use:** desk, settings-class surface — it lives with `modules/M01`'s tenant configuration pattern (`M01-58`) and is reached from there. In a small firm the Owner is also the coordinator, and this surface must work for one person holding both jobs. The toggle is moved one person at a time; it is never a bulk operation.

## Entry & exit

Reached from: the tenant configuration/settings area (the PRD places this surface with `modules/M01`'s tenant configuration pattern, `M01-58`, "reached from here" — `prd/modules/M09-field-workforce.md` §2 Surfaces). The toggle itself is per-employee, moved on one person's record at a time (`M09-11` context). Turning tracking on is never sold from a coordinator's dashboard — the decision is made on this Owner surface (`M09-61` context). Leads to: not pinned by PRD — designer decides, note the decision. (The usage ledger, invoices and proration this toggle feeds are `modules/M12`'s and are not surfaces of this screen.)

## Requirements (verbatim)

### prd/modules/M09-field-workforce.md

- **M09-04** (P0) — **The toggle is the billing switch, and `DD7` says which unit it moves: "Owner toggles tracking per employee; billed as tracked-seat-months in the usage ledger; per-seat price set per market book."** This module owns the toggle **as a product surface** — who may move it, what it says before it moves, what the employee sees afterwards (§M09.2). It owns none of the accounting: the usage ledger, the month-fraction arithmetic, the invoice line and the overage behaviour are `modules/M12-platform-billing.md`'s, and no figure, rate or proration rule appears in this document. _(non-UI half, build-side: billing unit is tracked-seat-months; ledger, proration and invoicing owned by M12 — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M09-12** (P0) — **Turning tracking on states, before it commits, that it starts a charge.** The confirmation names the person, names the five capabilities that become available for them (`M09-03`), and states plainly that this employee becomes a **tracked seat** billed as tracked-seat-months from this moment. It shows the tenant's current tracked-seat count before and after. It does **not** compute a price in this module: the surface reads the market book's per-seat price — presenting a draft value as draft (`M09-16`, owner ruling 2026-08-04 Q1/Q17) — and where a slot is empty it says so.
- **M09-14** (P0) — **Turning tracking off stops collection at once, and says what happens to what was already collected.** No further position, route, geofence event or movement record is produced for that employee from the moment the toggle moves. What was collected while they were tracked remains readable under the retention and rights rules of §M09.8 and §M09.10 — it is not deleted by the toggle, and the surface says so rather than letting a user assume either outcome. _(non-UI half, build-side: collection ceases immediately on toggle-off; collected data kept under retention rules — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M09-16** (P0) — **The per-seat price is market-book data, and the launch market's slot now carries the owner's draft value (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q1/Q17).** The IN book records **≈₹99/seat/mo beyond the tier's included allowance — Starter 0 / Growth 3 / Pro 10 / Enterprise custom included seats** (`BM-41`, `F1-61`), every number **draft pending rate-card verification** (`BM-17`/`BM-26`). Until verification, the toggle surface presents the price as draft and the meter as not yet sellable — it never shows a zero, never shows another market's number converted (`F1-26`), never treats a draft as final, and never quietly enables an unbillable capability as though it were free. Where any market's slot is genuinely empty, the empty-state honesty rule stands unchanged. _(non-UI half, build-side: price is market-book data; empty slot means not sellable; no FX conversion — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M09-44** (P0) — **The tracking window is concretized (owner ruling 2026-08-04, Q39): collection runs from the worker's own day-start tap to their day-end tap, worker-controlled, with a tenant force-stop backstop — default 20:00, owner-set — and no attendance means no tracking that day.** The window is never inferred from behaviour: the product never derives it from when a person usually works, never extends it because someone is still checked in past the force-stop (collection stops; the open check-in surfaces per `M09-24`), and never starts it without the person's own day-start mark. The force-stop hour renders on the tenant's timezone with the timezone named (`F1-10`, `F3-22`); the employee sees their live tracking state and its bounds on their own device — the **always-visible tracking indicator** (`M09-13`). Where a market's `pack.data-rights` determination sets a stricter rule, the pack wins (`M09-67`). The definition lives here: v1 ships no per-employee shift patterns (`M10-29`), so the tenant-wide force-stop plus worker-controlled marks is the whole law. _(non-UI half, build-side: window = day-start tap to day-end tap plus owner force-stop backstop (default 20:00); never inferred; stricter pack wins — for awareness, not for drawing)_
- **M09-50** (P0) — **A geofence is a radius around its anchor, set per site by the tenant, with a stated default and an honest minimum.** The default radius is a tenant setting; a site may override it; and the surface states what the radius means in the market's units (`pack.formats`). A radius smaller than the typical accuracy of a consumer position fix is refused with the reason named, because a fence the product cannot reliably tell you have crossed is a fence that generates false events (`M09-46`'s accuracy law applied). **P0 because two P0 rows depend on it:** `M09-51`'s prompt-never-acts law is only meaningful if the fence that raised the prompt is one the product can reliably tell was crossed, and `M09-49`'s anchor rule assumes a radius exists on the anchor it names — a geofence with no defined radius is not a narrower feature, it is a source of false events on a privacy-sensitive surface. _(non-UI half, build-side: radius below typical fix accuracy refused with reason named — for awareness, not for drawing)_ (This screen carries the **tenant default** radius setting; the per-site override is SCR-M09-07's.)
- **M09-68** (P0) — **A market without a data-rights determination cannot have tracking enabled in it at all.** `F1-05` already makes the privacy/residency determination a precondition of any tenant existing in a market; this module adds the consequence for its own most sensitive capability: absence of a determination is a **disable**, not a permissive default, and the tracking toggle in such a market states that tracking is unavailable pending the market's determination rather than being silently available. _(non-UI half, build-side: absent determination is a disable, never a permissive default (F1-05) — for awareness, not for drawing)_

## States

- **loading**
- **empty** (no employees to list yet)
- **error**
- **normal** — the per-employee toggle list with the tenant's tracked-seat count, the tenant default fence radius, and the force-stop hour (default 20:00) rendered on the tenant's timezone with the timezone named (`M09-04`, `M09-44`, `M09-50`).
- **enable-confirm-with-counts** — the cost-stating confirmation: names the person, the five capabilities, states tracked-seat billing as tracked-seat-months from this moment, shows the tracked-seat count before and after (`M09-12`).
- **disable-confirm-data-fate** — toggle-off confirmation: collection stops at once; already-collected data remains readable under retention and rights rules, and the surface says so rather than letting the user assume either outcome (`M09-14`).
- **draft-price** — the IN book's draft ≈₹99/seat/mo and the included-seat ladder presented as draft pending rate-card verification, never zero, never converted, never final (`M09-16`).
- **unpriced-market** — a market book with no per-seat price at all: the surface states that tracking is not yet priced and shows no number (`M09-16`).
- **tracking-unavailable-no-determination** — a market without a data-rights determination: the toggle states that tracking is unavailable pending the market's determination; never silently available (`M09-68`).

## Data volume

Design at a firm of ~20 field staff (the PRD's own scale: "An owner with twenty field staff moves twenty toggles, is told the count each time"). A mixed team — a few tracked, most not — is the expected shape. One tenant default radius, one force-stop hour.

## Numbers carrying provenance

- **Tracked-seat count before and after** the toggle commits (`M09-12`) — these are the same counts the product bills from (the row consumes `F8-33`); each carries its F8 provenance tier in the design.
- **Draft per-seat price ≈₹99/seat/mo** and the **included-seat ladder (Starter 0 / Growth 3 / Pro 10 / Enterprise custom)** (`M09-16`) — rendered as draft pending rate-card verification, never as a final sellable rate, never zero, never another market's number converted.
- **Force-stop hour (default 20:00)** (`M09-44`) — renders on the tenant's timezone with the timezone named.
- **Tenant default fence radius** and the **honest minimum** below which a radius is refused (`M09-50`) — stated in the market's units (`pack.formats`).
