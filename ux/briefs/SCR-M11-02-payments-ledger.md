# SCR-M11-02 · Payments Ledger

Work the tranche schedule and money ledger; the one surface where money is written.

**Module:** M11 · **Personas:** Finance (primary — owns money correctness), EPC Owner (everything, all money), Project Manager (records what arrived against the project they run, from the site), Sales Manager (the same, team-scoped), Sales Executive (read-only on their own won deals — enough to chase a customer), Operations (reads collections at portfolio scope) · **Context of use:** desktop carries the full ledger and the period view; mobile carries the two acts that happen away from a desk — record a payment with a receipt photograph, and send or copy a payment link or a request message (M11 §2 surface emphasis as amended by owner ruling 2026-08-06, Q45; it read "copy a payment link or a request message" before the ruling). Every money mutation is online-only and is refused rather than held.

## Entry & exit

Reached from: the project's money block (`M08-35`'s surface links into it) and from Finance's own home (SCR-M11-01, `M11-54`) — M11 §M11.8's behavior detail: "It is reachable from the project (`M08-35`) and from Finance's own home (`M11-54`); it is the same screen in both places." Every other surface in the product links into it rather than duplicating a control (`M11-52`). Leads to: Record Payment (SCR-M11-03) from the tranche row (M11 §M11.5 behavior detail: the recording sheet "is reachable from the tranche row on the payments screen"); the copy-payment-link and copy-request-message actions complete on this surface, and so does the send of the link and its request message from the tenant's connected transactional channel where one is connected (`M11-24`/`M11-26` as amended by owner ruling 2026-08-06, Q45; `M03-03`) — the message leaves the product from here rather than routing the operator anywhere else. The **plain request message on its own** completes here too, for any holder of project visibility including a read-only reader (owner ruling 2026-08-06, Q48; `F2.M11.send-request-message` — see the **reader-read-only** state); *this sentence is added by that ruling, which the entry-and-exit line predates.* Other exits: not pinned by PRD — designer decides, note the decision.

- **Error** (base).
- **Normal** — the tranche schedule; per row: state, amount, date and receipt; ledger entries beneath.

- **Send-on-connected-channel** — a transactional channel is connected: the due row's link and request message send from the tenant's own channel under the transactional template class, and the channel's delivery states ride the sent message honestly — exactly as that channel reports them and no further (`M11-26` as amended, `M03-03`, `F5-28`). The send is the primary act on this path; it moves a message, never money — settlement is unchanged and stays customer → the tenant's own account (`M11-01`). **Who reaches this state:** the link's send is a `F2.M11.record-payments` act, while the **plain request message's** send rides the actor's own project scope (`F2.M11.send-request-message`, `F2.M08.project-visibility`), so a holder of project visibility alone reaches this state for the message and never for the link — draw both paths (owner ruling 2026-08-06, Q48; see **reader-read-only**). **Every send from this state is audit-written under the sender's name (owner ruling 2026-08-06, Q52; `M11-07`, `F2-22`) — and the audit entry has no rendering here.** It is written behind the surface; this module has no audit-log screen (tenants read the log through `F2-23`'s export), so draw no "sent" chip, tick, sender line or any other affordance from it. ## Data volume

One project's schedule: typically 3–4 tranche rows (the platform seeds the 10/60/20/10 and 30/60/10 templates, `M01-54`), but each tranche may carry many payment entries (`M11-36`), reversal pairs render as pairs in the history (`M11-47`), and an earlier revised schedule must stay readable alongside the one in force (`M11-14`) — design the ledger to stay workable at a dozen-plus entries per project. Sales Executive and Operations read the same surface without the write actions.

## Numbers carrying provenance

Every user-visible number carries its F8 provenance tier in the design, and money carries its qualifiers at every density:

- Each tranche's percentage share and computed amount (the version's arithmetic — never typed).
- Each tranche's outstanding amount (what the due row still owes; what a link is minted for).
- Each ledger entry's amount, including negative reversal amounts.
- Each entry's received-on date; each tranche's state date (the date it entered its state).
- The collected-against-due figure (one computed figure; every surface shows the same one).
- The surplus amount where more was collected than the schedule expects.
- The superseded link's amount versus the current outstanding amount.
- The earlier (revised-away) schedule's amounts, still readable.
- The waived tranche's amount (never counted as collected).
- Qualifiers that ride every figure: confirmed-by-account vs hand-recorded (`M11-42` — never dropped), never final while stale.
