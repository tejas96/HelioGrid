Suryodaya Solar will supply, install and commission the rooftop solar system as specified in this proposal, including all balance-of-system components and DISCOM liaison for net metering.

Payments follow the tranche schedule in this proposal. Work on each phase begins once the corresponding tranche is received.

Manufacturer warranties apply to panels and inverters as listed. Workmanship is warranted for 5 years from commissioning.

Timelines are estimates. DISCOM approval and net-metering timelines are outside Suryodaya Solar's control and may extend the schedule. Delays caused by site access, weather or statutory approvals are excluded from any penalty.
5. Cancellation
Orders cancelled after material dispatch are subject to restocking and logistics charges. Advance payments are adjusted against costs already incurred.
