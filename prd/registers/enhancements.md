# Enhancements register

Purpose: every `REC` (recommended-enhancement) requirement in the suite, gathered in one place
with its rationale. `REC` items live in their module for context **and** here — they are never
mixed with source truth (`SRC`) or brief-mandated scope (`BRIEF`); this register is the reminder
that everything listed here is a recommendation, not a documented requirement from the corpus or
the owner's brief.

Row format:

| REC id | module | recommendation | rationale | tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `M06-33` | `modules/M06-proposals.md` §M06.5 | **Battery-economics modeling** — a storage-economics layer on the proposal's battery flow: savings contribution, backup-hours estimate, and tariff-window (time-of-use) arithmetic, rendered as labelled projections with disclosed assumptions (`F8-23`). | Design spec §10's competitive validation (2026-08-03) found rivals model storage economics deeply — backup duration, self-consumption, ToU arbitrage, battery-to-tariff ties — while the source keeps v1's battery flow transactional (capacity, chemistry, cost, tax, warranty — P0, `M06-30`). The spec mandates this register row verbatim: "battery economics modeling goes to the enhancements register as a REC." Not source truth and not brief scope — a recommendation only; adopting it must not touch the transactional flow's P0 status. | P2 |
| `M03-16` | `modules/M03-marketing.md` §M03.2 | **Multi-step nurture sequences** — a campaign form with several steps, waits between them, and branch-on-response, replacing this release's one-audience/one-content/one-send-moment campaign (`M03-12`). | The owner's brief asks for campaigns, capture and a pipeline hand-off — *"The platform should manage marketing campaigns, capture leads, and feed them into the sales pipeline"* (`_process/owner-brief-2026-08-03.md` §Marketing) — and says nothing about journeys or sequences. A sequence is not a bigger campaign: every step needs its own consent re-check (`M03-46`), its own suppression pass (`M03-47`) and its own metered burn (`M03-44`), and a wait between steps means the audience it sends to is not the audience that was approved. Recommended so the shape is on record; not source truth and not brief scope. | P2 |
| `M03-17` | `modules/M03-marketing.md` §M03.2 | **Split (A/B) testing of campaign content** — two content variants sent to slices of one audience, with the difference reported. | Standard in the category and a genuine improvement to send quality, but absent from the brief and constrained by this suite's honesty foundation: a variant comparison over a real audience is still correlation, so any such report would have to carry `F8-30`'s caveat exactly as `M03-53` does, and small audiences (the common case for an SME EPC) would produce differences the product must not present as conclusions. Recommended with those conditions attached; never presented as source or brief scope. | P2 |
| `M03-37` | `modules/M03-marketing.md` §M03.4 | **Automatic hand-off of a campaign capture into the voice agent's follow-up queue** — a campaign-captured lead queued for `modules/M07`'s agent without a person routing it. | The agent already exists (`SRC`, `modules/M07`) and campaign captures are exactly the enquiries it was built to chase, so the connection is the obvious next step — which is why it is recorded rather than left implicit. It is not brief scope: the brief asks that voice-AI follow-up *remain* part of the product, not that it be newly triggered by marketing. It would also create an automated outbound touch originating from a **marketing** consent rather than a customer-initiated enquiry, and would have to pass `modules/M07`'s non-swappable compliance gate (`M07-27`) unchanged — the gate is not negotiable for a marketing convenience. | P2 |

Two conditions travel with it: the market's AI-disclosure posture is the tiered law ruled 2026-08-04 (`Q6` — hard floors always; proactive disclosure per pack flag, `F1-36`(d)), and generated marketing copy would still have to clear whatever template registration the channel and market pack require (`M03-39`, `F1-38`) — a generator that produces unregisterable content is not a feature. | P2 |
| `M09-25` | `modules/M09-field-workforce.md` §M09.3 | **Lone-worker safety escalation** — an open check-in that passes a tenant-set duration on a site raises an escalation to that person's coordinator ("checked in at Sharma residence for 4 h 20 m, no check-out"), as a notification rather than an alarm. | The brief's ten capabilities are about visibility of the work, not safety of the worker, so this is outside brief scope and is recorded rather than assumed. Two conditions travel with it: it must reveal **nothing beyond the check-in the person themselves made**, so it stays available for untracked employees without becoming a location disclosure (`M09-03`); and the threshold must be a tenant setting rather than a product constant, because a four-hour install and a twenty-minute service call are not the same job. | P2 |

Three conditions travel with it: it is a **suggestion a person accepts**, never a re-assignment the product performs, because assignment authority is `modules/M02`/`modules/M04`'s and reordering someone's day is a management act; it must not become a productivity surface (`M09-09`); and it requires no tracked seat, since it plans from booked addresses rather than from observed positions — placing it on the included side of `DD7` and therefore outside the per-seat bundle (`M09-03`). | P2 |
| `M09-34` | `modules/M09-field-workforce.md` §M09.4 | **Customer-facing arrival window on the customer link** — surfacing "your technician is on the way, arriving within the hour" on the existing no-login customer link, derived from the assigned visit rather than from a live position. | The brief's field capabilities are internal — an EPC seeing its own field day — and nothing in it asks for a customer surface, so this is a recommendation. Three conditions travel with it: it must be derived from the **booked visit**, never from a live position, so no employee's location is disclosed to a customer under any circumstance (`M09-64`, `M09-66`); it must carry `foundations/F8`'s honesty on an estimate that will often be wrong; and it is orthogonal to register **`Q33`**'s transactional send channel (ruled 2026-08-04 — automatic from the connected channel) — the window would render *on* the link the customer already holds, not be sent to them. | P2 |
| `M09-63` | `modules/M09-field-workforce.md` §M09.9 | **Nearest-available dispatch** — answering *"who is nearest to this site right now"* as an action: proposing the nearest tracked, working, currently-free employee for an urgent visit and assigning it in one step. | `M09-43` already makes "who is nearest" the stated purpose of live location; turning the answer into an assignment is the obvious next step, which is exactly why it is recorded rather than left implicit. Three conditions travel with it: it works only for **tracked seats** and must render honestly for a mixed team rather than making untracked people invisible (`M09-61`); it must **propose and never auto-assign**, because assignment is a management act and an automatic one built on gapped position data would be a confident decision over an honest input (`M09-45`, `M09-09`); and the assignment itself would still be made by whichever module owns the work being assigned (`modules/M02`, `modules/M04`, `modules/M08`), not here. | P2 |
